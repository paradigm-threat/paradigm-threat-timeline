## N. A. Morozov (1854–1946)

Russian **mathematician** and **astronomer**; long **imprisonment**; seven-volume **astronomical** **critique** of **conventional** **ancient** **dates**.

**Morozov’s conclusions (his thesis, not this book’s creed):**

- Much of **classical** **antiquity** as dated is **illusion**.
- **Old Testament** **astronomy**, in his reading, **fits** **later** **composition** — **rejected** by **Jewish** and **Christian** **scholarship** that **defends** **antiquity**; recorded here as **part** of the **chronology** **wars**.
- **Greece** and **Rome** as **standardly** dated lack **matching** **astronomical** **records**.

He **influenced** Fomenko.

**Key works:** *Christ* (7 volumes, 1924–1932) — **Morozov’s** **series** **title**.
