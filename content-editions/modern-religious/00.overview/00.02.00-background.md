Challenging the **standard** historical timeline is old work. Since the **16th** century, critics such as [Jean Hardouin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_Hardouin), [Robert Baldauf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Baldauf), [Edwin Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edwin_Johnson_\(historian\)), [Wilhelm Kammeyer](http://alternative.shoutwiki.com/wiki/Chronology), [Heribert Illig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phantom_time_hypothesis), and [N. A. Morozov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikolai_Morozov) have argued that **church-backed chronologists** — including figures tied to the [Holy Roman Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holy_Roman_Empire) and to [Joseph Scaliger](https://chronologia.org/en/old_books/scaliger_petavius_about.html) — **reshaped** how **antiquity** was **dated**. **Fair** history requires naming **Roman** **institutional** **power** here — **councils**, **censures**, **crusade** **theology** — **without** **blaming** **today’s** **Catholic** **laity** for **medieval** **policy**.

[Anatoly Fomenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anatoly_Fomenko#Historical_revisionism) — mathematician, elected a full member of the Russian Academy of Sciences (1994) for mathematical work — applied statistics across thousands of texts (Bible, apocrypha, Quran, [Book of Mormon](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm?lang=eng), king-lists, and more). Professional historians in Russia and abroad reject his *New Chronology* as history; RAS standing is not state sponsorship of that project. Fomenko’s theses about Jesus and chronology are his hypotheses — not dogma for any church, mosque, or synagogue — but they illustrate how Latin and imperial record-keeping might have buried or encoded sacred history inside dating schemes ordinary believers never controlled.
