The narrative sketch: proto-planetary growth, capture, Brown Dwarf Saturn phase, assembly of worlds, Golden Age collinear order, then breakup and catastrophe spanning what this book labels the **Hebrew-scripture era** for shorthand, then stabilization into present orbits — **“New Testament era”** here is only a timeline partition label borrowed from Western chronology jargon, not a claim about Christian canon law for readers of other paths.

![Before the Golden Age, Proto-Saturn was a Brown Dwarf System floating through our galaxy.](/media/00.overview/brown_dwarf.jpg)

Proto-Earth was consumed by Proto-Saturn and carried into Sun's orbit — Earth's *conception* in this model. See [Before Creation](/timeline/evt-before-creation).

Unable to contain charging bodies within, the plasma sheath exploded, creating gas planets and pouring material into Sun's orbit. This coalesced around [Birkeland currents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birkeland_current) forming moons and rock planets, forcing all into [collinear configuration](http://qdl.scs-inc.us/2ndParty/Pages/11007.html). This high-energy period is the Golden Age — when all life forms are created.

Planets grow; the collinear system breaks into a non-linear “round-table” system marked by [epic cataclysms](https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/632271) spanning the Dark Ages (same era label as above). After instability resolves, planets violently break away to find final orbits. Recent millennia begin (timeline partition only).

After thousands of stable years, each planet loses density, drifts outward, eventually [rips](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Rip) out of the solar system. The Sun's system empties, ready for another cycle.

See [Thomas Chalmers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Chalmers), [G.H. Pember](https://archive.org/details/earthsearliestag00pembuoft), [Scofield Reference Bible](https://archive.org/details/holybiblescofield00scof).

Young-universe creationism and this model may overlap only on short human-history strands for some readers — exegesis remains open in every community.

**Other gods:** Hebrew Scripture forbids them as rivals in worship; Islamic teaching warns against *shirk*; each tradition maps “heavenly powers” onto its own theology. This book does not make mythic names co-eternal with the God of Abrahamic faith — or with Brahman, Nibbāna, or other ultimates in non-Abrahamic paths.
