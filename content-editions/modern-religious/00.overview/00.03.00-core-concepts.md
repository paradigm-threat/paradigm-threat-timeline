# Core Concepts — Eight Propositions (Modern Religious Edition)

> **TL;DR:** TODO

This reconstruction rests on eight testable claims about history and nature. Your theology rests on what your tradition holds as revelation; this book does not replace that. A reader may still ask whether earth history bears witness to catastrophe and reordering consistent with many sacred narratives — as **interpretation**, not as a new creed.

**Anchor for this book:** myths name Saturn, Venus, Mars and other figures as gods or powers in the sky. Here those are read as what nations feared and worshiped — forces in experience and cult — while **you** map that language onto tawhid, Trinity, Brahman, dharmakaya, or other categories in your own framework. Hebrew Scripture forbids worship of “other gods” (Exodus 20:3) as one classic form of that distinction; parallel warnings appear elsewhere (e.g. shirk in Islam).

## The eight propositions

### 1. Saturn as Earth’s original polar companion

Before **4,077** **BC**, **Saturn** stood in a **polar** **relationship** including **Venus** and **Mars** — **Golden** **Age** / **World** **Tree** **imagery**. **If false:** much **mythic** **mapping** **weakens**.

### 2. Earth’s year-length changed in steps

**225** → … → **365.24** **days** tied to **close** **encounters**. **If false:** many **calendar** **arguments** **fail**.

### 3. The solar system as more than gravity-only

**Charged** **bodies** and **large-scale** **electrical** **environment** are **hypothesized** as **mechanism** for **some** **catastrophes**. **Laboratory** **detail** is **omitted** **here**; see **canonical** **timeline**. **If false:** part of the **mechanical** **story** **drops**.

### 4. Ancient texts as sky-witness — read beside your tradition’s monotheism or ultimate Real

Myths describe battles of gods; here read as witness to terrifying sky events, while each reader affirms ultimate Reality as their path teaches. If false: the comparative-myth method loses force.

### 5. Multiple global catastrophes in human memory

**Floods**, **Venus/Mars** **eras**, **later** **shocks**. **If false:** the **chapter** **spine** **shakes**.

### 6. Dating methods need recalibration

If **year-length** and **flux** **changed**, **C-14** and **analogues** **need** **rethought**. **If false:** **standard** **dates** **stand**.

### 7. Conventional timeline extended by duplication / phantom time

**Illig**, **Fomenko**, **allies**. **If false:** **synchronization** **thesis** **needs** **repair**.

### 8. Rapid fossil formation (hypothesis)

**Canonical** **timeline** ties **this** to **electrical** **and** **lab** **claims**; **this** **edition** **defers** **proof** **to** **the** **full** **book** **and** **site**. **If false:** **deep-time** **paleontology** **as** **usually** **taught** **stands**.

**Propositions** **share** **one** **skeleton**: **configuration** **(1)** **seeds** **catastrophe** **(5)**; **variable** **year** **(2)** **supports** **(7)**; **(4)** **feeds** **dating** **(5)**; **(6)** **follows** **(2)**.

## What we do not claim

No requirement for young-earth creationism, literal acceptance of every Fomenko date, or identifying any founding figure of a living religion with a planetary body. No reliance here on ice-core or C-14 for pre–4077 BC in this model.
