# The Length of a Year Changes Throughout Antiquity

![Year-Length Through Antiquity](/media/00.overview/year-length-timeline.svg)

> **TL;DR:** TODO

{.book-illustration}

This reconstruction ties **Earth’s** **year-length** to **repeated** **celestial** **upheavals** — **not** to **gradual** **Newtonian** **drift** **alone**. **Time** **reckoning** in the model **begins** ~**4,077** **BC**; **steps** lead toward **365.24** **days**. The **Sumerian** **king** **list** is read with a **225-day** **hypothesis** (Cook and others).

**Note:** Biblical and other sacred events named below are aligned for comparison; dates are reconstruction, not commentary on inspiration or exegesis in any one community.

## Year-Length by Era

| Period (BC) | Days/year | Orbit (AU) | Era |
|-------------|-----------|------------|-----|
| Before 4,077 | — | ~0.72 | Pre-ignition: no time reckoning |
| 4,077–3,147 | 225 | 0.72 | Saturn ignition / Golden Age |
| 3,147–2,349 | 240 | 0.75 | Old Kingdom (post-flood, no Moon) |
| 2,349–2,193 | 260 | 0.79 | Fall of the Absu / Moon appears |
| 2,193–1,492 | 273 | 0.83 | Middle Kingdom |
| 1,492–747 | 360 | 0.99 | New Kingdom / Exodus era |
| 747 BC–today | 365.24 | 1.00 | Current era |

## Orbital jump mechanism (narrative)

| Date | Agent | Event |
|------|-------|-------|
| ~3,147 BC | Jupiter–Saturn collision | World Flood; 225→240 days |
| ~2,349 BC | Venus | Noah's Flood; Moon appears; 240→260 days |
| ~2,193 BC | Venus | Fall of Akkad; 260→273 days |
| ~1,492 BC | Venus (Central Pacific) | Exodus; 273→360 days |
| ~747 BC | Mars | Era of Nabonassar; 360→365.24 days |
