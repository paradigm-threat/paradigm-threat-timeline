# Timeline Synchronization — Creation Numbers and Redaction

Creation-era **traditions** **cluster** **differently**: **Septuagint**-related **counts** near **~5500** **BCE** versus **other** **indigenous** and **reconstructed** **zeros** near **~3100–4100** **BCE**. The **gap** is read here as **evidence** of **selective** **transmission**, **not** as **proof** **against** **inspiration**.

Jewish and Christian communities have never been uniform on genealogy arithmetic while confessing one Creator; both Septuagint and Masoretic strands deserve context when this book cites dates. Islamic and other calendars bring further legitimate variation — the aim is transparent comparison, not sectarian scorekeeping. Cook-style reconstruction maps year-zero choices to catastrophe chapters (4077, 3147, 2349, 685 BCE, etc.).
