## Lazeria-style polar maps

[Watch: Lazeria Map Collection — Is the Earth Hollow?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=izh1CSJL9SU&list=PLdYCrt6hIRKYlfT3yTqgUIZvnWkqsSN_e&index=48)

1500s–1700s navigational charts repeat four rivers flowing inward toward a central polar sea — not allegory, claims the collection.

Gerard Mercator (1569): North Pole as four islands and inward rivers; Latin note on waters draining inward. Grandson’s editions retain the motif.

Matthias Quad (1600) confirmed similar detail — and was banned by ecclesiastical authority that year, a fact worth weighing in any history of who controlled geographic narrative (see this edition’s institutional themes in the overview chapters).

Frisland appeared on many charts then vanished from orthodoxy.
