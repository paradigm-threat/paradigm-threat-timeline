# Three Lights in the Southern Sky

![3 Plasmoids in the Southern Hemisphere](/media/01.before-creation/3-plasmoids-in-the-southern-hemisphere.png)

> **TL;DR:** TODO

Before the Golden Age, southern peoples remembered a pillar of light and three great luminaries — [Hurakan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huracan) in [Popol Vuh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popol_Vuh), [Muspelheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muspelheim) in Norse cosmology, and parallel figures elsewhere. Petroglyphs worldwide repeat humanoid and column forms visible from southern vistas. Cook and Peratt read these as plasma discharge — full citation and IEEE papers sit on canonical `content/` and the site; this edition keeps the phenomenon as witnessed glory and terror, not as a physics lesson.

## Structure remembered in myth

Cook [Chapter 10](https://saturniancosmology.org/peratt.php) summarizes a south-polar column, three ball-like lights along it, and global rock-art concentrations. Duration claims before 4077 BC remain outside verifiable chronology ([Before Creation](/timeline/evt-before-creation)).

### Petroglyph pattern (descriptive)

- Carvings often high on cliffs with southern view
- Repeated recarving — as if the display returned
- Squatting humanoid and column motifs span continents

### Mythological parallels

| Form | Cultural parallels |
|---|---|
| Bright spheres | Genesis 1:3 · Maori/Hawaiian · Muspelheim · *Popol Vuh* · Hopi |
| Luminous humanoid | Adam Kadmon · Jomon · Purusha · Hurakan · Kokopelli |
| Creator creature (end-on) | Maya turtle · Phoenix · Khepre · Nazca figures |

Hurakan — “Heart of Sky” — related to “hurricane.” Muspelheim — southern realm of fire.

### Why the south

In Cook’s model, Earth sat below Saturn’s equator; current flow northward toward Saturn made the south the stage.
