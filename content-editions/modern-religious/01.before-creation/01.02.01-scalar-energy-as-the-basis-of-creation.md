## One thesis in plain language

The full timeline holds that high-energy electromagnetic ordering in the ancient sky — not random mutation alone — could bias matter into biological structure. In that reading, “after our image” in myth is not only metaphor: it may also be compressed memory of a real top-down imprint, whatever vocabulary each culture used for the powers above. Readers still anchor truth in God as Creator — in the terms of Torah, Gospel, Qur’an, or another path; the project’s secondary claim is that the nations’ stories and the physical model point the same direction — form given from the realm people experienced as heaven.

## Golems again: text, then speculation

In Scripture the *golem* word-family touches unformed life known to God before birth (Psalm 139:16). Later Jewish texts imagine shaped matter animated by command — the legendary golem — which rhymes with Genesis 2:7 and with myths of inert stuff suddenly becoming a man. The canonical scalar chapter uses “Golem” as a technical label for early structured bodies in that model (not as a claim that the Maharal’s tale is historical fact). This edition keeps the literary and biblical sense in view: OT-related discourse already had a place for “formed / unformed” and “quickened from above.” Whether those images map onto the timeline’s metal-and-field mechanism is exactly what the long version tries to spell out; here we only note continuity of theme.
