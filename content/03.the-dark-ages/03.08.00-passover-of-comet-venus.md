# Sodom & Gomorrah / 1492 BCE — "Passover" of Comet Venus & the Exodus

## 1936 BCE — Sodom & Gomorrah are completely Destroyed

**Sodom & Gomorrah (1936 BCE)** — With Jupiter's influence in decline and Venus absent, the nonlinear configuration destabilized. An alignment between Mars, Earth, and Mercury caused the complete destruction of the five cities of the plain (Sodom, Gomorrah, Admah, Zeboiim, Zoar). Flaming sulfur from Mars fell to Earth as a miles-wide column of electric discharge. The KJV would later redact this natural cataclysm into a story of divine retribution. Under powerful electrical discharge, elements can transmute; instant fossilization can occur during interplanetary discharge.

## 1492 BCE — "Passover" of Comet Venus & the Exodus

**Passover of Comet Venus (1492 BCE)** — In the last 675 years, gas-giant Jupiter has been directly exposed to the Sun's field drawing energy into it and growing larger. This eventually resulted in Jupiter flipping polarity and ejecting the consumed gas planet Venus. On April 19, 1492 B.C. Jupiter unexpectedly **expels Venus** as a massive Comet that **passes over** Earth's sky and heads towards the Sun. The passage of Venus in proximity to the earth resulted in further _plagues_. Seeing the approach of this "**angel of death**", the terrified population makes [ram sacrifices](https://biblehub.com/exodus/29-16.htm) and splashes the blood on the sides of their houses. Jno Cook's [synopsis](https://www.saturniancosmology.org/syn.php) places the blow in the east Central Pacific, an electric arc through the Pacific and Indian Ocean into the Mediterranean, and Moses' escape during the turmoil; the year length increased from 273 to 360 days. (For a difference between this timeline's view and Cook's regarding the Andes and sea-water traces in high lakes, see [Jno Cook — Differences](/timeline/evt-jno-cook#differences-from-this-timelines-view).)

## Year Jumps to 365 Days — Earth Leaves Jupiter's Orbit

Based on the increase in the number of days (+92) in a year, we can ascertain that Earth has finally left Jupiter's orbit and is on an independent orbit around the Sun. The _newly independent_ inner planets began to form final electro-magnetic fields. Full exposure to the Sun caused the Earth to become covered in obscuring clouds and a plague of darkness lasting 20 years.

N. A. Morozov, in *The Revelation in Storm and Thunder* (1907), pioneered reading scriptural narrative as encoding astronomical and natural phenomena rather than divine miracle. In that spirit, Alfred de Grazia (*God's Fire*, 1983) treats the Exodus as literal catastrophe: the plagues of Egypt, the pillar of cloud and fire, and the parting of the Red Sea as physically real events produced by cometary and electrical discharges in an electrically charged atmosphere — so that plagues such as frogs and locusts arise from the same feverish propagation of vermin under electrically charged, scorching conditions that others have described.
