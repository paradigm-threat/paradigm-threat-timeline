## What Were Pyramids Actually For?

Pyramids are found not just in Egypt but on every continent — across Mesoamerica (Maya, Aztec), China, Sudan, Bosnia, Indonesia, and the Pacific. They cluster along the equatorial band and at locations of concentrated electromagnetic energy. This global distribution cannot be explained by Fomenko's theory that they were Horde imperial monuments or Christian Easter-cake symbols — that does not account for pyramids predating Christianity and spanning cultures that had no contact with the Rus.
