# Wuxing and the Five Planets — Five Elements as Observational Record

Towards the end of the [Dark Ages](/timeline/evt-3147-bce-the-dark-ages), Earth shared the sky with four other bodies in a visible, nonlinear configuration: **Venus, Mars, Jupiter, and Mercury** — five planets circling and balancing each other, exchanging discharges and tidal effects. This timeline argues that **Wuxing** (五行, the classical "Five Phases" or "Five Elements") was developed in that period not as philosophy, religion, or martial art, but as the **observational record** of that configuration. The five elements were not speculative; they were what observers saw and whose causality they acknowledged.

## Five Planets, Five Elements

Each of the five bodies displayed a dominant element to terrestrial witnesses:

- **Mercury** — **Metal.** The planet most associated with metal; its appearance and role in electric and metallurgical lore (e.g. Hermes, Thoth, alchemy) align with a "metal" signature.
- **Mars** — **Fire.** Although Mars also carried metal (e.g. iron shell, [asteroid belt](/timeline/evt-march-23-priori-mars-loses-its)), fire was central — the red glow, plasma, and [Prometheus](/timeline/evt-mars-aka-prometheus-replaces-venus-as-the-new-saviour)-as-fire-giver.
- **Venus** — **Water.** Not because Venus was "made of" water, but because it was classified as the most **liquid** — fluid, flowing, comet-like; the element of flow and transformation.
- **Jupiter** — **Wood** (木). In this reading, wood stands for **electricity**, growth, and lightning — the great storm god, the king of the gods, the source of bolts and life-giving discharge.
- **Earth** — **Earth** (土). The ground underfoot; the stable centre from which the other four were observed.

Between the planets, observers could see **interactions**: arcs of lightning, plasma tails, and electromagnetic balance. The cycles of Wuxing (generating, controlling) map onto the cycles of how these five bodies acted on one another. At the time, all of this science was **visible in the sky** and could not be suppressed.

**Planets as combinations.** The planets were regarded as the elements *themselves* — not as "pure" fire or water. Each planet was a **combination of all elements**, with **one element as the head**. Wuxing asserts nothing about pure fire or water doing this or that; it asserts that **these planets** did things, and that the **interactions between planets** gave clues about the lower elements (fire, water, earth, metal, wood). The real nature of Wuxing is combination with one element at the head.

## Chaos, Meteorology, and Respect for All Gods

The late Dark Ages were a time of great chaos and meteorology — interplanetary lightning, year-length changes, and successive "saviour" planets that became destroyers. No human could know which god would next appear to strike them down or to intervene. The one who had **departed** (Saturn, then Jupiter, then Venus in turn) remained in ritual and memory. Wuxing, in this reading, is the **history of planetary configurations** between the [Golden Age](/timeline/evt-4077-bce-the-golden-age) and the current age: a record of which bodies were in the sky and how they balanced and opposed each other — and a reminder to respect all of them, including the one who had left.

## Not Philosophy, Religion, or Martial Art

Mainstream scholarship often treats Wuxing as a Warring States–era philosophy or a framework for medicine, statecraft, and martial arts. This timeline does not deny later uses but proposes an **earlier, observational origin**: Wuxing began as the codification of what was seen when five planets shared the sky. Philosophy, religion, and martial application came later, building on a cosmology that was once direct observation.
