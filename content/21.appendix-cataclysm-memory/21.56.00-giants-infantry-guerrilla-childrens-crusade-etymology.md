## Mainstream etymology (reference)

**Infantry** — [Online Etymology Dictionary: infantry](https://www.etymonline.com/word/infantry): 1570s, French *infanterie*, Italian/Spanish *infanteria* “foot soldiers… too inexperienced or low in rank to be cavalry,” from *infante* “foot soldier,” originally “a youth,” from Latin *infantem* (see *infant*).

**Guerrilla** — [Online Etymology Dictionary: guerrilla](https://www.etymonline.com/word/guerrilla): diminutive of Spanish *guerra* “war”; English from Peninsular War context.

**Infant / infante fork** — Italian *infante* “foot soldier” vs Spanish/Portuguese **Infante/Infanta** (royal prince[ss]); same Latin root, divergent military vs court meaning.
