## Napoleon and the Financial Revolution

Napoleon's relationship to Europe's financial restructuring was paradoxical. He abolished the [Inquisition in Venice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inquisition) in 1806, liberated Jewish populations, and promoted their legal equality. Gueniffey: Napoleon promoted Jewish assimilation into the French nation. His central bank project, however, competed directly with the Bank of England's debt-based system. His [Continental System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continental_System) — an economic blockade of British goods — aimed to destroy the City of London's financial dominance. This is the real reason Britain never stopped fighting him. The Napoleonic Wars were a war between two financial systems: property-based order versus debt-based empire.

## The Napoleon–Britain Paradox

If Napoleon was genuinely at war with Britain, his actions make no sense. In 1803 he sold [Louisiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisiana_Purchase) — the heartland of North America — to Britain's own American colony for a fraction of its value. He assembled a "Grand Army of England" on the Channel coast for two years (1803–1805) and never launched it. The [Battle of Trafalgar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Trafalgar) (1805) is offered as the explanation — but if a united continent truly sought to invade England, there were a hundred miles of coastline and no permanent fortification. Naval superiority does not explain why Napoleon *never attempted a crossing* and then chose to march 2,000 miles east into Russia instead.

Hitler repeated the identical pattern in 1940–41: he assembled Operation Sea Lion, postponed it indefinitely, and turned the entire Wehrmacht east into Russia — Reverse Crusade III. Historians must invoke "hubris" or "miscalculation" twice, for two different men in two different centuries, to explain the same inexplicable strategic decision. The simpler answer: **England was never the real target because England housed the command structure.** Both Napoleon and Hitler were directed by the financial networks centered on the City of London. Their assignment was always the same — destroy the last successor of the Horde.

## The Continental and Anti-Continental Coalitions

France faced **seven successive coalitions** organized by Britain and financed by the City of London. Each was broken militarily by Napoleon; each was reassembled through new British subsidies. The pattern reveals the asymmetry: Napoleon could win every battle and still lose, because his enemy's primary weapon was credit, not armies.

The Romanov-British partnership is critical context: the dynasty that partitioned Moscow Tartary now served as the eastern arm of British containment. The 1812 Russian campaign resulted not from military genius but deliberate scorched-earth strategy combined with climate — and the impossibility of occupying Moscow without triggering full Romanov-British resource mobilization.

## The Aftermath: Restored Order and Managed States

The [Congress of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congress_of_Vienna) (1814–1815) restored European monarchies with one crucial difference: every restored monarchy now operated within a framework of national debt to international banking houses (principally the Rothschilds, who had funded multiple sides and emerged as dominant European financial power). Political independence was real; financial independence was not. The post-Napoleonic order was the Westphalian system completed: sovereign states whose sovereignty operated within boundaries set by their creditors.
