## Structural Observations

The antibody phenomenon is not limited to individuals. **Operations** can function as antibodies — actions taken by factions within the system to sabotage the system's own plans. If the good-actor thesis on 9/11 is correct (see [False Flags](/timeline/evt-false-flags-architecture)), it represents the largest antibody action in modern history: not a single whistleblower but a coordinated counter–false flag that permanently altered the Deep State's operational landscape.

The Pyramidal Empire's response to antibodies follows a consistent escalation:

1. **Ignore** — if the information seems obscure (Velikovsky initially)
2. **Discredit** — attack credentials, character, sanity (Webb, Velikovsky)
3. **Isolate** — remove institutional support, funding, platform (Tesla, Webb)
4. **Imprison** — when the information cannot be contained (Vanunu, Ellsberg's indictment)
5. **Kill** — when all else fails (Bruno, Cooper, possibly Webb)

Encoding (Lewis) bypasses this escalation entirely because the information is never recognised as a threat. This suggests that **the most effective antibody strategy is invisible encoding in culturally protected forms** — fiction, children's literature, religious texts, music. The information survives because destroying the container would destroy something the culture values.
