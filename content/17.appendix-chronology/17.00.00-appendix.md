## Reader feedback and corrections (book-facing)

### FB-001 — Anatoly Fomenko: “state-sponsored historian” (retracted)

A reader of the **Russian translation** reported that calling Anatoly Fomenko a *state-sponsored historian* is **misleading**: he is a **recognized mathematician**, elected **full member of the Russian Academy of Sciences (1994)** for **mathematics**, while his and G.V. Nosovsky’s **New Chronology** writings are **rejected** by **professional historians** in Russia and internationally—i.e. the “official” historical profession’s stance is **hostile**, not sponsoring. The reviewer added that **New Chronology** was not treated as state-backed project work (no grants named; Soviet-era **denunciations** alleged but **no** systematic persecution described).

**Correction applied:** The same wording was removed from paradigm-threat-files [`history/chronology/page.md`](https://github.com/clevertree/paradigm-threat-files/blob/master/history/chronology/page.md) and from the timeline overview [`content/00.overview/00.02.00-background.md`](https://github.com/clevertree/paradigm-threat-timeline/blob/main/content/00.overview/00.02.00-background.md); both now separate **RAS membership (mathematics)** from **mainstream rejection of *New Chronology***. Regenerate **DOCX/PDF** (and any Russian export) so compiled books pick up the fix.

**Second thread (not “fixed,” recorded):** The same feedback bundled **Saturnian / 4077 BCE / catastrophism** with **New Chronology** and read the mix as **fantasy or esotericism** rather than science—the “fly in the ointment” problem. This chronology **already** treats **Fomenko** and **Saturnian** lines as **different frameworks** in project rules; the actionable item is **clearer chapter boundaries** in any **compiled book** so readers who want **only** statistical chronology are not forced through **cosmology** in the same breath.

**Open:** Pass the same Fomenko wording correction through any **standalone Russian book** source if it was not generated from `page.md`. Optional non-Western anchor for “academy vs. pseudohistory”: RAS digest *В защиту науки* (“In defence of science”) bulletins on [ras.ru](http://www.ras.ru/digest/fdigestlist/bulletin.aspx) (see Wikipedia article on Fomenko for pointers).
