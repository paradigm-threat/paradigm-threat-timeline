# Censorship and Book Burning: From Alexandria to the Internet

Every regime destroys the previous era's records — fire, edict, index, algorithm. Whoever controls the past controls the present. The method changes; the function never varies. Key episodes: Library of Alexandria, Qin Shi Huang, Roman censorship, Index Librorum Prohibitorum, de Landa's Mayan codices, Scaliger/Petavius reframing, Bolshevik and Nazi destruction, Operation Mockingbird, digital platform censorship. The investigator must assume every surviving record has been filtered; absence of evidence is not evidence of absence; redundancy is survival.
