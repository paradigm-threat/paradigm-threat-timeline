# Historical Antibodies: Independent Agents Against Erasure

An **antibody** is an independent agent who preserved suppressed knowledge or exposed covert operations through personal courage — not [controlled opposition](/timeline/evt-controlled-opposition-managed-disclosure). Asymmetry: a single person permanently alters the information landscape. Key examples: C.S. Lewis (encoded cosmology in Narnia; shield: children's author), Daniel Ellsberg (Pentagon Papers; Gulf of Tonkin), Smedley Butler (Business Plot), Velikovsky, Tesla, Vanunu, Webb, Cooper, Bruno. The more direct the exposure, the more severe the response — from Lewis (died natural causes) to Bruno (burned alive). If the good-actor thesis is correct, 9/11 may be the largest antibody operation in modern history.
