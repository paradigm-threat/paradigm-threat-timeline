# 2000 CE — 21st Century: The Final Struggle — Reverse Crusade IV

The 21st century marks the culmination of every thread in this timeline. What began as a hidden priest class hoarding knowledge has metastasized into a global technocratic grid of finance, surveillance, and narrative control. But the same history that reveals this architecture reveals its weakness: **the Pyramidal Empire has created its own antibodies.**

The current geopolitical confrontation — NATO expansion eastward, the Ukraine proxy war, economic sanctions designed to isolate Russia — is **Reverse Crusade IV**: the latest iteration of the same pattern that began in [1812](/timeline/evt-napoleon-invades-russia) (Reverse Crusade I), continued through [1914](/timeline/evt-the-1917-revolution) (Reverse Crusade II) and [1941](/timeline/evt-the-1917-revolution) (Reverse Crusade III), and now operates through financial and proxy mechanisms rather than direct invasion. The objective has not changed: elimination of the last sovereign successor of the Horde and absorption of its territory into the debt-based financial order.

## The Immune Response

Certain leaders have always disrupted Pyramidal Empire operations. In the 21st century the immune response has reached critical mass:

- **Whistleblowers** have documented Pyramidal Empire operations with unprecedented specificity
- **Decentralized technology** has broken information distribution monopolies

Compartmentalization that protected secrecy now prevents coordination:

- **Traditionalist factions** within intelligence agencies leaking information

The result: visible chaos at the highest levels — contradictory policy, public elite infighting, exposure of hidden networks, loss of narrative control.

## Preparation for the Event

Some leaders appear to understand that certain things **cannot be stopped** — they fight to ensure humanity survives:
- Relocating critical infrastructure from coastal regions
- Hardening supply chains against electromagnetic disruption

The nature of "the event" remains disputed — solar micronova, Mars orbital return, pole shifts, plasma discharge. What unites these theories: **Earth's cosmic environment is not stable**, and the last 200 years of apparent stability are the exception.

## The WW3 Blueprint: Best of Both Wars

The Deep State's plan for WW3 was not a repetition of one previous war — it was **a hybrid of the two most successful elements from WW1 and WW2**, combined into a single architecture.

From **WW1**: the trigger mechanism. WW1 proved that you do not need to build a case for war over years — you need **one spectacular act of terrorism** that activates a pre-built cascade. Sarajevo 1914 turned a single assassination into a continental war within weeks because the alliance architecture was already in place. The WW3 blueprint replicated this: build the NATO alliance architecture to Russia's borders, arm Ukraine to the teeth, and wait for (or engineer) a single terrorism event that activates the cascade. The [9/11 thesis](/timeline/evt-false-flags-architecture) argues that good actors pre-empted this trigger by executing it early and publicly, converting the script from covert holy war to overt War on Terror.

From **WW2**: the disposable figurehead. WW2 proved that a media-manufactured leader can be built up, used, and then discarded — taking all blame with him when the operation fails. Hitler was [published, promoted, and financed by Western institutions](/timeline/evt-world-war-ii-reverse-crusade-iii), then retroactively recast as the enemy the West had "always opposed." The WW3 blueprint assigned this role to **Zelensky**: a television actor elevated to head of state through a Western-backed colour revolution, armed by NATO, celebrated by every Western media outlet — and ultimately expendable. When the proxy war fails, Zelensky absorbs the blame. The West "never wanted escalation." The architects remain invisible. The script is identical to the post-WW2 pivot: Nuremberg trials, NATO formation, historical revision — all designed to ensure the planners are never implicated.
