# 2000 CE — 21st Century: The Final Struggle — Reverse Crusade IV

The 21st century marks the culmination of every thread in this timeline. What began as a hidden priest class hoarding knowledge has metastasized into a global technocratic grid of finance, surveillance, and narrative control. But the same history that reveals this architecture reveals its weakness: **the Pyramidal Empire has created its own antibodies.**

The current geopolitical confrontation — NATO expansion eastward, the Ukraine proxy war, economic sanctions designed to isolate Russia — is **Reverse Crusade IV**: the latest iteration of the same pattern that began in [1812](/timeline/evt-napoleon-1812-campaign) (Reverse Crusade I), continued through [1914](/timeline/evt-bolshevik-revolution-fulfilment-of) (Reverse Crusade II) and [1941](/timeline/evt-bolshevik-revolution-fulfilment-of) (Reverse Crusade III), and now operates through financial and proxy mechanisms rather than direct invasion. The objective has not changed: elimination of the last sovereign successor of the Horde and absorption of its territory into the debt-based financial order.

## The Immune Response

Certain leaders have always disrupted Pyramidal Empire operations. In the 21st century the immune response has reached critical mass:
- **Independent researchers** have exposed Fomenko's chronology, Electric Universe physics, and Saturnian cosmology outside institutional channels
- **Whistleblowers** have documented Pyramidal Empire operations with unprecedented specificity
- **Decentralized technology** has broken information distribution monopolies
- **Certain world leaders** have openly resisted the global financial system

## The Infighting Begins

Compartmentalization that protected secrecy now prevents coordination:
- **Financier factions** seeking indefinite debt expansion
- **Technocrat factions** pursuing total surveillance
- **Ideological factions** pursuing cultural destabilization without economic planning
- **Traditionalist factions** within intelligence agencies leaking information

The result: visible chaos at the highest levels — contradictory policy, public elite infighting, exposure of hidden networks, loss of narrative control. ## Preparation for the Event

Some leaders appear to understand that certain things **cannot be stopped** — they fight to ensure humanity survives:
- Relocating critical infrastructure from coastal regions
- Hardening supply chains against electromagnetic disruption
- Preserving knowledge outside vulnerable digital systems
- Re-establishing local governance capable of operating without centralized control
- Diplomatic de-escalation to prevent nuclear war compounding natural catastrophe

The nature of "the event" remains disputed — solar micronova, Mars orbital return, pole shifts, plasma discharge. What unites these theories: **Earth's cosmic environment is not stable**, and the last 200 years of apparent stability are the exception.

## The WW3 Blueprint: Best of Both Wars

The Deep State's plan for WW3 was not a repetition of one previous war — it was **a hybrid of the two most successful elements from WW1 and WW2**, combined into a single architecture.

From **WW1**: the trigger mechanism. WW1 proved that you do not need to build a case for war over years — you need **one spectacular act of terrorism** that activates a pre-built cascade. Sarajevo 1914 turned a single assassination into a continental war within weeks because the alliance architecture was already in place. The WW3 blueprint replicated this: build the NATO alliance architecture to Russia's borders, arm Ukraine to the teeth, and wait for (or engineer) a single terrorism event that activates the cascade. The [9/11 thesis](/timeline/evt-false-flags-architecture) argues that good actors pre-empted this trigger by executing it early and publicly, converting the script from covert holy war to overt War on Terror.

From **WW2**: the disposable figurehead. WW2 proved that a media-manufactured leader can be built up, used, and then discarded — taking all blame with him when the operation fails. Hitler was [published, promoted, and financed by Western institutions](/timeline/evt-world-war-ii-reverse-crusade-iii), then retroactively recast as the enemy the West had "always opposed." The WW3 blueprint assigned this role to **Zelensky**: a television actor elevated to head of state through a Western-backed colour revolution, armed by NATO, celebrated by every Western media outlet — and ultimately expendable. When the proxy war fails, Zelensky absorbs the blame. The West "never wanted escalation." The architects remain invisible. The script is identical to the post-WW2 pivot: Nuremberg trials, NATO formation, historical revision — all designed to ensure the planners are never implicated.

The entire architecture depended on **one precondition**: a unified Western bloc willing to escalate in lockstep. Every NATO member had to maintain sanctions. Every Western media outlet had to maintain the narrative. Every Western government had to funnel weapons without public dissent. The alliance cascade — the lesson of WW1 — only works if **every node in the network fires when triggered**.

### Why Trump Broke the Plan

This is why Trump broke the plan.

Trump's return to power shattered the unified Western front that the WW3 blueprint required. A U.S. president who questioned NATO's purpose, demanded European defence spending, pursued direct communication with Russia, and refused to follow the escalation script made the cascade impossible. The architecture was built for a Clinton, a Biden, a Macron — leaders who would follow the playbook without deviation. Without a unified West, the Sarajevo-style trigger cannot activate. Without unified media, the Zelensky brand cannot be maintained. Without unified escalation, the proxy war becomes a frozen conflict rather than a continental conflagration. The WW3 blueprint — the most sophisticated version of the Reverse Crusade, combining the best innovations of 1914 and 1941 — was structurally defeated by the one variable it could not control: an uncompromised leader inside the alliance who refused to fire when triggered.

## The Disclosure Pattern

The Pyramidal Empire operates by **"Revelation of the Method"** — announcing plans publicly before executing them, obtaining implied consent from a population that fails to resist. In the 21st century this has accelerated to absurdity: pandemic simulations months before pandemics, financial collapse simulations before collapses, fictional portrayals of technologies before deployment.

But the tool now works against them. The population recognizes "predictive programming" so readily that disclosure no longer functions as consent — it functions as **warning**. This may be the Pyramidal Empire's single greatest tactical loss.
