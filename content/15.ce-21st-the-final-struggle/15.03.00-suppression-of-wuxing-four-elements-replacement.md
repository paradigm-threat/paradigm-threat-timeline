# Suppression of Wuxing — Four Elements, Air, and the Erasure of Planetary Science

![Suppression of Wuxing — Five phases replaced by four elements and air](/media/15.ce-21st-the-final-struggle/wuxing-suppression.png)

> **TL;DR:** Wuxing ties five sky phases to five dominant planets and their coupled causality; communist-era suppression in China and Western four-elements-plus-air dogma overwrote that grid and severed the observational spine. Once the post–Dark Ages sky stabilized, the old weather went quiet—then policy and Greek-style “elements” made forgetting easy and nearly complete.

{.book-illustration}

[Wuxing](/timeline/evt-wuxing-five-planets-five-elements) — the Five Phases rooted in the [Dark Ages](/timeline/evt-3147-bce-the-dark-ages) observation of five planets and their causal interactions — was suppressed in mainland China under CCP communism and replaced in the West by a different system: **four elements**, with **air** included as an element even though it is not one in the Wuxing framework. The replacement eliminated the nature of causality between elements, the science, the history, and the link between the five phases and the five bodies that had once been visible in the sky.

## CCP Suppression and the Bruce Lee Legend

In China, traditions that preserved Wuxing as a living practice — including lineages of female martial artists who had operated and transmitted it for over 300 years — were targeted under communist cultural policy. The story of **Bruce Lee** is often tied to these legends: he is said to have taken or carried the legends of those female martial artists and the Wuxing-based arts into the West. Suddenly, in China, the living tradition was gone: suppressed, scattered, or driven underground. In the West, what arrived was fragmentary — and the dominant Western framework was already the Greek-derived **four elements** (earth, air, fire, water), not five. Western insistence on four elements and on **air** as an element overwrote the five-phase system. Causality between elements, the observational basis, and the planetary link were dropped; Wuxing was treated as philosophy or superstition, with no real science behind it.

## Why Forgetting Was Easy — After the Dark Ages

Once the [solar system had stabilized](/timeline/evt-684-bce-solar-system-becomes-stable) and the [planets had departed](/timeline/evt-684-bce-solar-system-becomes-stable) to their current orbits, the conditions that had made Wuxing obvious disappeared. The sensitivities of the meteorologists — the ability to read sky and space weather — faded. There was no more space weather in the old sense, no more cataclysm, no more visible interplanetary causality. With nothing in the sky to anchor the five phases, it became easy for everyone to forget Wuxing as **science** and to treat it only as philosophy, hypothetical, something with no real basis. Suppression under the CCP and replacement by the four-element model in the West then completed the erasure.

## Wuxing as Solar-System Science

This timeline argues that Wuxing is essentially **the science of our solar system** — not because five is a magic number, but because that is how things turned out here. We had five planets in the relevant configuration and five causalities. In a solar system with a different number of dominant bodies (a scenario that may not be all that possible elsewhere), things would be different: colors, the number of digits on a hand, and much else would align to whatever number that system settled on. As energy flows through the planets, the **high scalarity** of five planets sharing energy at the same time breaks down into **micro-scalarity** and affects every life form in the solar system. Wuxing, in that reading, is the codified record of that scalar structure — and its suppression is the suppression of that science.

## The Real Nature of Wuxing — Combinations, Not Pure Elements

A crucial distinction: in the original Wuxing framework, the **planets were regarded as the elements themselves** — not as “pure” fire, pure water, or the like. Each planet was a **combination of all elements**, with **one element as the head**. Nothing else is asserted: Wuxing does not claim that “fire” and “water” as abstract absolutes do such-and-such. It claims that **these planets** did things, and that the **interactions between planets** gave clues about the lower-level elements — fire, water, earth, metal, wood. The real nature of Wuxing is therefore **combination of elements with one element at the head** — a planetary, observational fact — not a set of claims about pure substances. The four-element replacement, and the insertion of “air,” erased that structure and with it the causality and the history.

*Author's thesis:* The suppression of Wuxing under the CCP and its replacement in the West by the four elements (including air) removed the last widespread link between the five-phase system and its observational, planetary basis. Restoring Wuxing means restoring it as the science of a five-planet configuration — and as a system of combinations with one element at the head, not as a philosophy of pure elements.
