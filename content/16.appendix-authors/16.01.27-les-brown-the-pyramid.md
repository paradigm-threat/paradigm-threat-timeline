# Les Brown (*The Pyramid*)

![Les Brown — The Pyramid (1978)](/media/16.appendix/les-brown-pyramid.png)

**Canadian** | **Pyramid greenhouse experimenter, interior designer turned farmer**

Les Brown published *The Pyramid: How to Build It, How to Use It* (Bancroft, Ontario, **1978**). He retired from Toronto interior design to a farm near Bancroft, built a **large Cheops-proportional wooden pyramid** (**1974**) for **crop production**, and promoted **pyramid geometry** for **food yield**, **preservation**, and miscellaneous **wellness** anecdotes. The work sits in the same **1970s “pyramid power”** class as commercial handbooks (magnetic/cosmic/radio speculation, **von Däniken**–style ET framing, **Atlantis** tropes).

**This timeline cites Brown** for **farm-scale pyramid greenhouse** culture and **Cheops-DIY** practice—**supporting evidence** only for reading pyramids as **purpose-built life-support shells** (food, air, continuity) at **[ley / EM nodes](/timeline/evt-pyramids-myths-vs-reality)**, alongside [Kerrell & Goggin](/timeline/evt-bill-kerrell-kathy-goggin). His **ET chronology** and **36× yield** claims are **not** adopted as chronology or agronomy proof.

**Boundary:** Effect claims are **not** established physics. The **2005** web reprint (Craig / Emily Cragg) adds **Faraday-cage** foundation notes and **electrosmog** language—**editorial**, not original 1978 imprint text throughout.

**Key work:** *The Pyramid: How to Build It, How to Use It* (1978)
