# Bill Kerrell & Kathy Goggin

![Bill Kerrell and Kathy Goggin — pyramid energy handbook (1977)](/media/16.appendix/kerrell-goggin.png)

**American** | **Pyramid energy research, popular instrumentation, commerce (Pyramid Power V, Inc.)**

Bill Kerrell (University of California, Berkeley; background in physics, electronics, photography) and Kathleen Goggin coordinated pyramid-model experiments from the early 1970s and published *The Guide to Pyramid Energy* (Forces, Santa Monica, **1977**). The bulk of the book is **1970s U.S. “pyramid power”** (models, food, sleep, blades, biofeedback, dowsing). **This timeline foregrounds** their **Great Pyramid** material on **habitability-oriented design**—especially **Brunton**-style **ventilation, access, and chamber** arguments that the monument fails as a naive sealed tomb—and uses that thread as **supporting evidence** for pyramids as **civilizational anchors** in the **[Dark Ages BCE](/timeline/evt-3147-bce-the-dark-ages)** (safest occupied nodes), alongside [Pyramids: Myths vs Reality](/timeline/evt-pyramids-myths-vs-reality).

**Boundary:** The authors ran a pyramid-products company; modern effect claims are **not** established physics. The **structural / layout** arguments are **second-hand** (Brunton) and must be checked against primary surveys.

**Key work:** *The Guide to Pyramid Energy* (1977)
