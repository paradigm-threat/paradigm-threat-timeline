# Bill Kerrell & Kathy Goggin

![Bill Kerrell and Kathy Goggin — pyramid energy handbook (1977)](/media/16.appendix/kerrell-goggin.png)

**American** | **Pyramid energy research, popular instrumentation, commerce (Pyramid Power V, Inc.)**

Bill Kerrell (University of California, Berkeley; background in physics, electronics, photography) and Kathleen Goggin coordinated pyramid-model experiments from the early 1970s and published *The Guide to Pyramid Energy* (Forces, Santa Monica, **1977**). The book is a **handbook** of claimed home and garden effects (alignment to magnetic north, open-frame vs covered models, food and blade tests, biofeedback anecdotes, dowsing material via Verne Cameron / Bill Cox, and a long numerology chapter on the Great Pyramid). It belongs to the **1970s U.S. “pyramid power”** wave — **not** to the same evidence class as Fomenko’s textual chronology or Davidovich’s geopolymer chemistry, but it is a **dated primary** for what experimenters said they saw and sold.

**Boundary:** The authors ran a pyramid-products company; claims are **not** established physics. This timeline cites them for **cultural and experimental-history** context, linked to the [pyramid energy investigation](https://paradigmthreat.net/history/chronology/investigations/pyramid-energy-kerrell-goggin-1977-investigation.md).

**Key work:** *The Guide to Pyramid Energy* (1977)
