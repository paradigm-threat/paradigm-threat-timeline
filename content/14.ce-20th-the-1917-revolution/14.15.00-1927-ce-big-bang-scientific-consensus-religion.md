# 1927 CE — Big Bang as Secular Creation Myth and the Scientist Priesthood

> **TL;DR:** Once telescopes resolved **objects outside the solar system**, a **creation-from-nothing** story **scaled up** to fill the newly visible “universe.” Georges Lemaître (Catholic priest) supplied the theology; **Hubble’s recession law** supplied the liturgy; **1965 microwave background** supplied the sacrament. The secular side **lost** the fight over whether the cosmos has a beginning — and inherited a **new priesthood**: **modern scientists**, speaking as **“the scientific consensus.”**

This timeline entry records **author sentiment** from field debate (June 2025): mainstream Big Bang cosmology is not treated here as measured history but as **dogma** — a thinly veiled **creation myth** dressed in mathematics after the sky map outgrew the solar system.

## The scaling move

Before the 1920s, public cosmology still largely **orbited the Sun**. “Universe” in practice meant **our system** plus fixed stars. When **spiral nebulae** were shown to be **external galaxies** (debate effectively closed by Hubble and others, ~1923–1929), the old **one-beginning** narrative did not die — it **expanded**:

- A **singular origin** (primeval atom / hot dense state)
- **Light before structured stars** (reframed as CMB after 1965)
- **Ex nihilo** language retained, now in **tensor calculus**

The move rhymes with [New Religion of Constancy](/timeline/evt-new-religion-of-constancy): deny the **chain of sky-saviours**, assert an **eternal fixed order** — here, an **eternal expansion law** administered by institutions.
