## Iran: documented U.S.–Khomeini contacts

- **Guardian (2016):** Carter administration **extensive contact** with Khomeini before the revolution; secret Paris meetings from **15 January 1979** (Zimmermann / Yazdi).
- **Khomeini to Carter (27 Jan 1979):** Promised protection of "America's interests and citizens" if Washington pressured the military to allow his return.
- **General Huyser:** Dispatched to **prevent** a military coup to save the Shah—effectively clearing the path for Khomeini.
- **Double game:** MI6/CIA cooperation with Tehran documented into the **1980s**; CIA later supplied lists of Soviet agents in Iran while also funding anti-Khomeini exiles ([Intel Today](https://inteltoday.eu); [CIA Reading Room — Khomeini](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/)).

**Open question:** Orchestrated revolution vs. **exploited** uprising—the distinction matters for the revival thesis. See [CIA investigation — Iran pipeline](https://paradigmthreat.net/governance/war/investigations/cia-investigation.md).

## Mecca: siege and Saudi pivot

On **20 November 1979**, Juhayman al-Utaybi seized the **Grand Mosque** in Mecca (~200–600 militants). Saudi forces, with French GIGN assistance, retook it by **4 December**; Juhayman and 68 others were executed.

**Consequences:** Humiliated Saudi Arabia turned to **Wahhabism** for legitimacy—stricter Sharia, export of ideology, bans on cinemas and music. Before 1979, Saudi had been relatively modern on social norms.

**Khomeini** falsely accused the U.S. and Israel of orchestrating the siege, triggering anti-American riots across the Muslim world. No direct CIA link to the siege is documented in open sources—**ongoing**.

## Poland: "We want God"

John Paul II's **June 1979** visit—the first pope in a communist-ruled nation—drew roughly **11 million** of **36 million** Poles. Warsaw's Victory Square: *"We want God."* Widely credited as catalytic for **Solidarity (1980)** and the later fall of the Iron Curtain.

Vatican–Western intelligence alliance against communism is historical fact; whether the **1979 visit** was coordinated with CIA remains **undocumented** here.
