# Mars Literature as Reclassified Nonfiction

> **TL;DR:** The ~1938 library **fiction** quarantine is read as technocratic relabeling of Mars testimony once printing and classification sat with revolution-consolidated states; listed pre- and post-Wells titles are treated as **nonfiction filed into fiction**, not genre invention alone. Separately: Wells’s *War of the Worlds* physiology is **not** claimed as a cut manuscript (AU-084 self-sealing host/occupant read).

Around 1938, the "fiction" category appeared in libraries across the world — shortly after major communist revolutions had consolidated power. Works that had been presented as factual or documentary were retroactively reclassified. This timeline argues the shift was deliberate: communism, using technological advantage (printing, classification systems, institutional control), became the primary instrument for suppressing knowledge. Real history was quarantined as entertainment.

## Stories this project believes were real history filed into fiction

**Scope note:** “Redacted into fiction” here means **genre quarantine** (libraries/publishers shelving testimony as entertainment) — not that every listed book had pages cut. For *War of the Worlds* specifically, see AU-084: [bipeds as occupants / self-sealing print](/cosmos/mars/investigations/wotw-martian-analysis.md#bipeds-as-occupants--the-provisions-cover).

- **Percy Greg**, *Across the Zodiac* (1880) — Mars inhabited by humans, hidden polar hierarchies, dictatorial theocracy. Presented as a recovered manuscript.
- **Robert Cromie**, *A Plunge into Space* (1890) — Human-like Martians, utopian society. Eight years before Wells.
- **Gustavus W. Pope**, *Journey to Mars* (1894) — Human Martians, cities, multiple races. A physician, not a writer; sources unclear.
- **Kurd Lasswitz**, *Two Planets* (1897) — Superior but benign Martians, contact at the North Pole. German; predates Wells by one year.
- **Garrett P. Serviss**, *Edison’s Conquest of Mars* (1898) — Immediate Earth **counterstrike** encode: electrical / anti-grav fleet, Mars strike, **canal waterworks destroyed**. Read with Primrose Hill leftover flyer as **high-likelihood secret war** filed into romance, not empty pulp ([secret war event](/timeline/evt-1898-1945-ce-earth-mars-secret-war); [*The Secret War*](/cosmos/mars/the-secret-war.md)).
- **Edgar Rice Burroughs**, Barsoom series (1912–1943) — Eleven novels: human Martians, forbidden polar zones, layered conspiracies, religious fraud as governance. Structural detail exceeds what Lowell's canal theory could have supplied.
- **C.S. Lewis**, *Out of the Silent Planet* (1938) — Mars (Malacandra) inhabited by three rational species under Oyarsa. Encodes medieval planetary cosmology. Published the same year the fiction category institutionalised the quarantine.
- **Leigh Brackett** and **Ray Bradbury** — Dying Mars, ancient civilizations, canals. Post-1938 but preserve the pre-inversion consensus.

The **[1896–1897 mystery airship wave](/timeline/evt-1896-1897-mystery-airship-wave)** precedes the Wells/Lasswitz cluster in the same decade: mass U.S. press under **airship** vocabulary (because *UFO* did not exist yet), then **Mars invasion and contact fiction** within months — read here as **sky episode → literary encoding → later genre quarantine**. [Mystery airships — vocabulary bridge](/influence/controlled_opposition/mystery-airships-1896-1897-vocabulary-bridge.md).

A [forensic survey of prefaces](/influence/predictive_programming/fiction_encoding/fiction-presented-as-fact-investigation.md) shows Defoe, Swift, Stoker, Tolkien, Lovecraft framed works as factual documents. **Swift:** *Gulliver's Travels* (1726) is treated as an early instance—travel-hoax memoir later shelved as satire—with Brobdingnag / Tartary–scale gunpowder chronology and Laputa’s paired Martian satellites developed on the timeline at [1726 CE: *Gulliver's Travels*](/timeline/evt-1726-ce-gullivers-travels-managed-disclosure) and in the [Swift investigation hub](/influence/predictive_programming/fiction_encoding/swift/page.md). Stoker's *Dracula* (1897) presents as fact with realistic physics; the magic came from later films.

**Author's thesis:** Communism did not merely oppose capitalism — it became the operational framework for suppressing all knowledge. Control of printing presses, libraries, and classification systems allowed real testimony to be relabelled "fiction" at scale. The 1938 institutionalisation of the fiction category was not neutral taxonomy; it was a technological coup. Authors who had documented Mars, blood science, and hidden hierarchies found their work shelved alongside escapist fantasy. The genre itself is the quarantine.
