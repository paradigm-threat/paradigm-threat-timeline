# Mars Literature as Reclassified Nonfiction

Around 1938, the "fiction" category appeared in libraries across the world — shortly after major communist revolutions had consolidated power. Works that had been presented as factual or documentary were retroactively reclassified. This timeline argues the shift was deliberate: communism, using technological advantage (printing, classification systems, institutional control), became the primary instrument for suppressing knowledge. Real history was quarantined as entertainment.

## Stories this project believes were real history redacted into fiction

- **Percy Greg**, *Across the Zodiac* (1880) — Mars inhabited by humans, hidden polar hierarchies, dictatorial theocracy. Presented as a recovered manuscript.
- **Robert Cromie**, *A Plunge into Space* (1890) — Human-like Martians, utopian society. Eight years before Wells.
- **Gustavus W. Pope**, *Journey to Mars* (1894) — Human Martians, cities, multiple races. A physician, not a writer; sources unclear.
- **Kurd Lasswitz**, *Two Planets* (1897) — Superior but benign Martians, contact at the North Pole. German; predates Wells by one year.
- **Edgar Rice Burroughs**, Barsoom series (1912–1943) — Eleven novels: human Martians, forbidden polar zones, layered conspiracies, religious fraud as governance. Structural detail exceeds what Lowell's canal theory could have supplied.
- **C.S. Lewis**, *Out of the Silent Planet* (1938) — Mars (Malacandra) inhabited by three rational species under Oyarsa. Encodes medieval planetary cosmology. Published the same year the fiction category institutionalised the quarantine.
- **Leigh Brackett** and **Ray Bradbury** — Dying Mars, ancient civilizations, canals. Post-1938 but preserve the pre-inversion consensus.

A [forensic survey of prefaces](/influence/predictive_programming/fiction_encoding/fiction-presented-as-fact-investigation.md) shows Defoe, Swift, Stoker, Tolkien, Lovecraft framed works as factual documents. Stoker's *Dracula* (1897) presents as fact with realistic physics; the magic came from later films.

**Author's thesis:** Communism did not merely oppose capitalism — it became the operational framework for suppressing all knowledge. Control of printing presses, libraries, and classification systems allowed real testimony to be relabelled "fiction" at scale. The 1938 institutionalisation of the fiction category was not neutral taxonomy; it was a technological coup. Authors who had documented Mars, blood science, and hidden hierarchies found their work shelved alongside escapist fantasy. The genre itself is the quarantine.
