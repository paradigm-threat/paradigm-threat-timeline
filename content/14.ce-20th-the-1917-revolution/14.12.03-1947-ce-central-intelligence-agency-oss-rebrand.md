# 1947 CE — Central Intelligence Agency: OSS Rebrand and Cold War Deep State

The **Central Intelligence Agency (CIA)** was created by the National Security Act of 1947, formally replacing and expanding the wartime **Office of Strategic Services (OSS)**. Conventional history treats it as the civilian foreign-intelligence arm of the United States. This timeline treats its origins, funding, and long-term role as an **open investigation**: OSS rebrand, alleged appropriation of Nazi gold and assets, creation largely **outside** American constitutional and republican oversight, and operation as a self-funding structure—drug trade, human trafficking, and covert commerce—that grew into what critics describe as a **massive parasite** on the state.

## OSS to CIA: Continuity, Not Clean Break

The OSS (1942–1945) ran espionage, sabotage, and resistance coordination; many of its personnel and networks flowed directly into the CIA. The “rebrand” was legal and bureaucratic, not operational. Pre-1947 activity—Nazi tracking, asset recovery, and British (MI6) cooperation—shaped the Agency’s culture and methods. Allegations that OSS/CIA elements captured or redirected Nazi gold and other assets during and after the war are documented in the [CIA Investigation](/governance/war/investigations/cia-investigation.md).

## Outside the Constitution

The CIA was given broad authorities and minimal public accountability: no declaration of war for its operations, no transparent line-item for much of its budget, and no electorate to answer to. The investigation examines the thesis that it was designed to operate **outside** the normal constraints of a republic—funding and enriching itself off the books while being protected by classification, disinformation, and institutional loyalty.

## Iran: Shah, Nukes, Ayatollah

The Agency’s hand in **Iran** is well documented for the 1950s: Operation Ajax (1953), restoration of the Shah, and support for nuclear-related infrastructure. The investigation traces the thesis that the same Anglo-American intelligence cooperation that built up the Shah and nuclear stations later helped engineer conditions for the **Ayatollah** to take over—so that nuclear assets would fall into the hands of radical extremists, creating an existential narrative and a possible “revelation of the method” (CIA-declassified intelligence suggesting handover of nuclear weapons to terrorist proxies) to justify a global end-game. The investigation also records the **author's opinion**: that the world has been blind to the Iranian nuclear threat above all because of the **fatwa** (nuclear bombs declared haram)—everyone assumes Iranians would never break it—while the real risk is **depleted uranium** (stored as "peaceful" byproduct), widely misunderstood and weaponisable with minimal technology, and that the CIA may have set up an underground facility to convert it. See [CIA Investigation](/governance/war/investigations/cia-investigation.md).

## Latin America: Gangs, Noriega, Maduro

Over decades, the CIA is alleged to have built or empowered criminal and paramilitary networks across Latin America—removing “good” actors and leaving “bad” ones in place—so that figures like **Noriega** (Bush Sr.) and **Maduro** (Trump-era) became targets of direct US action. The pattern is read as a long-term deep-state project with the Agency at the centre; the struggle to reverse it is read as **within** government, between old bureaucracy and “antibodies.”

## Antibodies: Defeat from Within

The only effective resistance, in this reading, came from **antibodies**—insiders who turned on the plan. **Daniel Ellsberg** (Pentagon Papers) is the exemplar: after participating in the system, he sought political cover and disclosure, helping to create space for scrutiny and new agencies. The investigation documents the thesis that the CIA was ultimately **defeated by people inside the CIA** who no longer wanted to be part of it and who used documents and political cover to create or empower new structures.

## 9/11: Disconnecting the CIA from Its Plans

This timeline sponsors a **good-actor thesis** for 9/11: that the attack was used—or executed—**against** the Deep State. [False Flags: Architecture](/timeline/evt-false-flags-architecture): good actors pre-empted a slow-burn Holy War; **WTC 7** (which housed a CIA secret station) was destroyed; the War on Terror was made **public**, not covert. Claims that the **NSA** or allied actors “took out” the CIA by bringing down Building 7, and post-9/11 statements by CIA representatives that the Agency was **diminished**, are collected in the [CIA Investigation](/governance/war/investigations/cia-investigation.md). The thesis: 9/11 was triggered early on purpose to disconnect the CIA from its long-term plans; new agencies under the War on Terror would “stomp out” those plans over time, in the US and abroad.

## Post-9/11 Crises: Tracing to the CIA

Almost every major American crisis since 9/11—from near–civil war and near-insurrection to government capture by media and institutional narratives (e.g. under the guise of science and vaccines)—is under investigation for **traceability to CIA-linked elements**. The investigation will map personnel, programmes, and narrative production to the Agency where evidence allows.
