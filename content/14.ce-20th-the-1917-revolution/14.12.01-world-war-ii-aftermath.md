# 1945– CE — World War II Aftermath

Allied firebombing destroyed irreplaceable pre-modern architecture across Dresden, Hamburg, Cologne, Tokyo, Osaka — architectural erasure complementing archival destruction. An alternative account holds elements of the German military-scientific complex evacuated (Neuschwabenland, Base 211); Operation Highjump (1946–47) deployed 4,700 personnel to Antarctica. The Vril question remains open — denazification-era document destruction created a permanent evidence gap. Mars testimony (Telegram SSP veteran) describes German bases on Mars. An open investigation documents "twin-tower" biological control technology across pharaonic symbolism and science fiction.
