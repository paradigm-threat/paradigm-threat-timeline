# 1880–1996 CE — Mars Literature as Reclassified Nonfiction

Between 1880 and 1938, authors across five countries independently described the same Mars: inhabited by humans, governed by hidden polar hierarchies. Their books were retroactively reclassified as "science fiction" — a genre that did not exist when most were written. A [forensic survey of prefaces](https://paradigm-threat.net/timeline?view=browser&path=investigations/fiction-encoding/fiction-presented-as-fact-investigation.md) shows Defoe, Swift, Stoker, Burroughs, Tolkien, Lovecraft framed works as factual documents. Stoker's *Dracula* (1897) presents as fact with realistic physics; the magic came from later films.
