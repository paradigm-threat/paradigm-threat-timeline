# CIA vs NSA: SIGINT Antibody, Tonkin Reckoning, and *Tron*

> **TL;DR:** After the [CIA's 1947 rebrand](/timeline/evt-1947-ce-central-intelligence-agency-oss-rebrand), Truman stood up the **NSA (1952)** as the **SIGINT** lane—legally separate from CIA **HUMINT** and covert action. Vietnam exposed **Project MINARET** and the **Church Committee**; **Gulf of Tonkin** became the hinge (complicit SIGINT, then **Ellsberg** and **Robert Hanyok** as antibodies). By the **1980s**, the corpus reads NSA as **watchdog of homeland communications** against CIA–MI6 proxy setups; Disney's ***Tron* (1982)** encodes that fight—**TRON** audits the **MCP** (CIA). Downstream: [false flags](/timeline/evt-false-flags-architecture-of), [historical antibodies](/timeline/evt-historical-antibodies), [*Ghost War* PP](https://paradigmthreat.net/influence/hollywood/ghost-war-the-antibody-phase.md).

## 1947: HUMINT lane already open

The [Central Intelligence Agency](/timeline/evt-1947-ce-central-intelligence-agency-oss-rebrand) inherited OSS networks, British (MI6) cooperation, and a culture of covert action outside normal republican oversight. This chapter is not a second CIA origin story; it tracks the **parallel institution** built to monitor **communications** rather than to run proxies abroad. Full OSS-to-CIA dossier: [CIA investigation](https://paradigmthreat.net/governance/war/investigations/cia-investigation.md).

## 1952: NSA and the SIGINT split

President Truman created the **National Security Agency** under **NSC Intelligence Directive 9** (1952), unifying military **communications intelligence (SIGINT)** and succeeding the Armed Forces Security Agency ([NSA.gov](https://www.nsa.gov/), [NARA RG 457](https://www.archives.gov/research/guide-fed-records/groups/457.html)). Open mandate: foreign SIGINT, cryptography, protection of U.S. communications—**not** CIA human intelligence or regime-change operations.

**Corpus read:** Structural separation is not proof of permanent rivalry, but it is the **architecture** an **antibody faction** could inhabit. Mockingbird-era media capture ([Mockingbird: Public Erasure](/timeline/evt-mockingbird-public-erasure)) ran on a different track from the wire-tap grid NSA would come to audit.

## 1964 and 1971: Tonkin and the document antibody

In August 1964 the Johnson administration sold a **second** North Vietnamese attack in the Gulf of Tonkin; Congress passed the **Tonkin Gulf Resolution**. **Daniel Ellsberg** (1971) released the *Pentagon Papers*, showing that successive administrations had expanded Vietnam while misrepresenting aims—an **external** rupture from inside the policy machine. See [Daniel Ellsberg](/timeline/evt-historical-antibodies) and [False Flags: Architecture](/timeline/evt-false-flags-architecture-of).

**SIGINT record:** NSA historian **Robert J. Hanyok** completed a classified study in **2001** documenting that midlevel NSA officers **skewed** intercepts and that the **second attack did not occur** as sold; after a **2002–2005** declassification fight, NSA released Hanyok's article and related material ([NSA release](https://www.nsa.gov/Portals/75/documents/news-features/declassified-documents/gulf-of-tonkin/articles/foia_requests/)). That is the **institutional** antibody parallel to Ellsberg—SIGINT truth pushed against the war party from **inside** the signals agency. Tier tables and chronology: [good-actor investigation § II.I](https://paradigmthreat.net/events/false_flag/investigations/good-actor-theory-interagency-antibody-investigation.md); [NSA investigation § III](https://paradigmthreat.net/governance/war/investigations/nsa-investigation.md).

**21st-century release window (2000–2005):** The same era stamps **`Approved For Release 2000/08/08`** on the CIA Reading Room [**MARS EXPLORATION, 22 May 1984**](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP96-00788R001900760001-9.pdf) **while** the **Tonkin** bundle stays **blocked until Nov–Dec 2005** — **parallel file fights** over **classified reality**. Author read: **NSA antibody pressure** may have **eased** the **STARGATE** Mars worksheet (**politically cheaper**); **Tonkin** took **~five years** after Hanyok’s audit because it **pierces the casus belli**. Full calendar: [**2000–2005 CE: NSA Tonkin vs Mars declass fight**](/timeline/evt-2000-2005-ce-nsa-tonkin-mars-declass-fight).

## 1967–1978: MINARET, Church Committee, FISA

**Project MINARET** (1967–1973) was an NSA watch-list program intercepting international communications of U.S. citizens and organizations—names supplied largely by **FBI, CIA, and DoD**. Targets included **Martin Luther King Jr.**, **Muhammad Ali**, journalists, and senators **Frank Church** and **Howard Baker** ([Church Committee Book III — NSA](https://aarclibrary.org/publib/church/reports/book3/pdf/ChurchB3_10_NSA.pdf); [Foreign Policy on Minaret](https://foreignpolicy.com/2013/09/25/secret-cold-war-documents-reveal-nsa-spied-on-senators/)).

The **Church Committee** (1975) exposed MINARET and related abuses. **FISA (1978)** and the Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Court followed.

**Author read:** Unlike CIA covert-action culture that MINARET **fed**, the **NSA institution** underwent a visible reckoning—shed much of the most dubious domestic watch-list role and refocused toward **SIGINT discipline** and **audit of traffic** rather than **setting people up abroad**, work the CIA already owned. MINARET facts are **documented**; "NSA cleaned up while CIA did not" is an **interpretive pattern**. See [NSA investigation § II](https://paradigmthreat.net/governance/war/investigations/nsa-investigation.md).

## 1980s: Watchdog on the wire

**Author thesis:** Sometime in the **1980s**, NSA leadership oriented toward **monitoring homeland communications** so opaque operators could not run with **complete exclusivity** in the emerging digital layer—and toward **not duplicating CIA foreign setups**. **SIGINT** may have surfaced evidence that the **CIA–MI6 lane** was **setting the world up** for a **slow-burn religious holy war** (see the [1979 religious revival](/timeline/evt-1979-religious-revival-holy-war-geometry) board laid the same year). That read is **speculative** until primary analytic product surfaces; it is **pattern-consistent** with the [good-actor eighty-year arc](https://paradigmthreat.net/events/false_flag/investigations/good-actor-theory-interagency-antibody-investigation.md).
