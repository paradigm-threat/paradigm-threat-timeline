## The Rebound Theory

In an [Electric Universe](/timeline/evt-1774-ce-the-mars-catastrophe), energy cannot be discharged at a target and simply absorbed. Planets are not solid spheres but **hollow capacitors** with multiple concentric shells.

When the Martians fired at Earth, energy entered the surface and propagated through its hollow shells. Each shell functions as a capacitor layer, charging to capacity before excess passes inward. The entire planet "charged up" like a massive electrical reservoir.

When Earth could absorb no more, the energy discharged outward through the poles. With Mars in **opposition** (closest approach), the path of least resistance was **back to Mars**. The weapon meant to subjugate Earth became the instrument of Mars's devastation. The melted city ruins photographed on Mars today bear witness: structures subjected to intense plasma discharge.

## The Carbon Dating Problem

If Mars (and portions of Earth) were recently incinerated — within the last thousand years — why does radiometric dating show "billions of years"? Because carbon dating cannot record a massive recent energy spike as recent. A catastrophic event incinerating all organic material resets the carbon clock to zero: no living carbon remains to begin the decay sequence. What survives is mineral carbon — locked in rock and glass — which reads as "very old" by default. Mainstream science concludes both planets are billions of years old, when the surfaces we photograph may have been melted within recorded human memory. The question is not whether the damage is ancient or recent — it is whether the dating method can distinguish the two.
