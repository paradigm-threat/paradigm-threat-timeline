# Bloodlands: Holodomor–Holocaust Continuum (1932–1945)

> **TL;DR:** The **same borderlands** (Ukraine, Belarus, Poland, Baltics) absorbed **Soviet famine terror (1932–33)** and **Nazi genocide (1941–45)**. Western memory **privileges** the latter’s **German-branded camp imagery**; Soviet memory **suppressed** the former. Timothy Snyder’s *Bloodlands* (~**14M** non-combat killings under **both** regimes) is the mainstream bridge — **not** a claim that the events are **one** catastrophe or **one** perpetrator.
