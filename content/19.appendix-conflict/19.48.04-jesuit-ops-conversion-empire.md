## The Founding

The Society of Jesus was established in 1534 by [Ignatius of Loyola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ignatius_of_Loyola) with papal approval in 1540. Unlike monastic orders, it was *operational*: mobile, linguistically trained, organized along quasi-military lines. Ignatius's [*Spiritual Exercises*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spiritual_Exercises_of_Ignatius_of_Loyola) produced operatives who were intellectually rigorous, culturally adaptive, and willing to die.

The Jesuits deserve respect despite serving purposes they often did not understand. Unlike Protestant chronologists who fabricated timelines, Jesuits genuinely documented what they found — languages, histories, cosmologies, astronomical knowledge — even when it contradicted Catholic narrative. This makes the Jesuit archive one of the most valuable sources for reconstructing pre-colonial world history.

## The Methodology

1. **Learn the language** — creating first dictionaries and grammars of hundreds of indigenous languages
2. **Document the culture** — religion, cosmology, medicine, governance recorded in annual reports
3. **Build trust** — schools, hospitals, agricultural assistance
4. **Identify the local deity** — almost universally the Sun — map it onto Jesus Christ
5. **Redirect worship** — gradually replace indigenous ritual with Catholic sacrament

When communities resisted, missionaries were killed, their deaths publicized across Europe as evidence of "savage" behavior, creating grounds for military intervention. The Jesuits took the punishment; the empire used their suffering to justify conquest.

## Converting Native Religions to Monotheism

**Older, oral populations** accepted Sun = Jesus at face value. **Younger, literate populations** were taught Jesus was the invisible *Son* of *Creator God Yahweh* (= Saturn), severing the connection to the observable sky permanently. Where persuasion failed, [military operations](https://sofrep.com/news/soldiers-god-catholicisms-special-operations-force/) were deployed.

## Jesuits Reach America

The 1492 Crusade carried Jesuits westward into Mesoamerica, where populations' astronomical knowledge often surpassed Europe's. The surviving [Maya codices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maya_codices) exist because Jesuits and Franciscans preserved them while colonial administration destroyed the rest. [Diego de Landa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diego_de_Landa) burned hundreds of Maya books in 1562, yet wrote the *Relación de las cosas de Yucatán* — the most important European source on Maya civilization. Documenting what you simultaneously annihilate is the Jesuit paradox.

The [Guaraní Reductions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesuit_reduction) — autonomous communities of up to 150,000 under Jesuit governance, protected from slavers — were by colonial standards remarkably humane, and for precisely that reason, destroyed by the Treaty of Madrid (1750).

## 1814 restoration and the Protestant capture of the archive

### What the missions actually built

Across the Americas, Asia, and Africa, Jesuit missions combined conversion pressure with **serious linguistics and ethnography**: grammars, *Relations*, star knowledge, plant lore, and political sketch maps. Where time allowed, fathers often **negotiated** local rites into a Catholic frame instead of smashing them on first contact. The honor of that work—whatever its complicity with empire—is that much of the archive preserves **indigenous content** that later regimes preferred to forget.

### Hijack of the Russian–Jesuit synthesis

This chronology holds that **Protestant** scholarly and **financial** networks did not merely disagree with Rome; they **inherited** the practical output of the Romanov–Jesuit world—atlas plates, route knowledge, ethnographic files—after that output had been laundered through courts and trading houses. “Russian” cartography and chronography in the eighteenth century already mixed Horde, Romanov, and Jesuit labor; the nineteenth-century **deep state** sat in a position to **relabel** that synthesis as **neutral science** while stripping its Hordian frame. In this reading, restored provinces had to **teach** and **cite** within a landscape of **Protestant-standard** histories and **Protestant-edited** world maps, whatever Roman loyalty individual provincials maintained.
