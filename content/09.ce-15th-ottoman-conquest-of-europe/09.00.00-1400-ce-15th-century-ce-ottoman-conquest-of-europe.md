# 1400 CE — 15th Century C.E. Ottoman Conquest of Europe.

![15th Century Ottoman Conquest of Europe](/media/09.ce-15th-ottoman-conquest-of-europe/15th-conquest-of-europe.png)

The religious schism in Europe continued to grow into the 15th century with no clear solutions available.
Once again, this allowed the Deep State to exploit the situation laying the foundations for the *next war*.
They just needed to find a *good reason* to trigger it.

By the middle of the 15th century, the popularity of
Dionysian [orgies](https://vermiliongoldfishartblog.wordpress.com/2017/06/02/pompeii-pornography-power/) had led to the
widespread proliferation of transmissible disease. Knowledge of disease and medicine was still limited, and
superstitious populations would regard the spread of illness with suspicion, often exiling the sick. The Empire had a
serious problem and didn't know how to solve it. It was once again time for the **Deep State** to step in and take
advantage. The fear of wide-spread disease could be exploited to bring Europe into a new Crusade. They had the
*reason* to go to war-now they needed the *means*. The Jesuits — the [Secret Society of Jesus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monita_Secreta) — became the primary instrument for spreading reformed monotheism globally. ## Meteorite falls on Yaroslavl (1421 CE)

In 1421, a meteor fell near [Yaroslavl](https://tmora.org/online-exhibitions/transcendent-art-icons-from-yaroslavl-russia/introduction-yaroslavl-city-of-the-bear/) leaving behind a giant meteorite of heavy metals. Hordian craftsmen found that when used as an additive, it produced strong resilient [Bulat](https://pegasus-leaders.com/bulat/) Steel — giving the Rus-Horde a new war advantage. During campaigns the Hordians and Atamans (Ottomans) carried the debris in a holy *arc* called Kaaba. See [Fall of Constantinople](/timeline/evt-fall-of-czar-grad-constantinople) for the 1453 siege that concluded the Hundred Years' War — conventionally framed as "England vs. France" but in New Chronology, Western Europe's failed resistance against the Russia-Horde.
