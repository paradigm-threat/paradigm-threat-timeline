Ancient testimony from every civilization agrees: there was no history — no *time* — before "creation." All accounts describe the pre–Golden Age as lasting darkness. Saturn's dwarf system was receiving its first energy from the Sun, and with that energy came the first life forms capable of memory. As Saturn had not yet joined the Sun's orbit, there was no mechanism for reckoning time.

## Saturn as a Brown Dwarf Star

According to Jno Cook's *Recovering the Lost World* ([Chapter 2: A Synopsis](https://saturniancosmology.org/syn.php)), Earth was not always a planet of our Sun. From genesis, Earth was a satellite of Saturn — then a **sub-brown dwarf star** wandering through space. Saturn generated faint infrared light and plasma output, sustaining the Saturnian System far from solar influence. Venus, Neptune, Uranus, and Jupiter were not yet formed, nor were Mercury or Mars. All planets reached their final form through the electronic bond forming between our Sun and Brown Dwarf Saturn.

## The Long Darkness: A Universal Memory

Cross-cultural mythology from every continent converges on one memory: before "creation," the sky was formless, dark, and chaotic. As Cook writes in [Chapter 13: The Creation](https://saturniancosmology.org/polar.php):

> *"It was universally held that the past before 'creation' was a long period during which the skies were in chaos and featureless turmoil."*

Egypt, Mesopotamia, Mesoamerica, India, Scandinavia, and indigenous traditions worldwide corroborate this. The myths describe a real observational condition: Earth was enveloped inside Saturn's **plasma coma** in **glow mode**, obscuring all stars and the Sun. From the surface, nothing was visible but swirling luminous mist.

Dwardu Cardona's *God Star* (2006) traces these "chaos" descriptions across mythological traditions, noting the primordial condition was typically described as a hovering cloud or ocean of mist with a twisted vapour column reaching sky to Earth — consistent with plasma discharge in glow mode.

## Life Awakens in the South

Nearly all life on Earth spawned during this "before creation" period — a very slow and gradual increase in energy as Saturn approached the Sun. The moment of creation is remembered by those who were *created* in it precisely because they were created much earlier.

In the **southern hemisphere**, a great source of light and power persisted throughout the before-creation time: the **Peratt column** — a plasma discharge stretching from Earth's South Pole into space, with three luminous ball plasmoids visible in the sky. This southern display (remembered as Hurakan, Muspelheim, the "three stones," and the turtle in Maya cosmology) provided such intense energy that it slowly awakened the sleepy, gradual evolution of each life form. It jumpstarted a period of rapid evolution, with humans reaching their final stable forms — organs, skin, and the bodies we recognize today — near the beginning of the Golden Age.

Those who witnessed "creation" in 4077 BC were not created in that moment. They had already evolved. What they witnessed was the *start of time* — the first moment that could be recorded.

## Cook's Dates and the Problem of Timekeeping

Cook proposes a sequence of events before 4077 BC: Earth entering Saturn's coma (~10,900 BC), settling into sub-polar orbit (~8347 BC), and the Cosmic Egg becoming visible through thinning clouds (~4900 BC). His 10,900 BC figure comes from correlating with the "Younger Dryas" period in ice-core records — but we do not support radiocarbon or ice-core dating as reliable. Even if a cold period is recorded in ice, it is not clear when it happened, why, or how long it lasted from such methods alone. These events (coma, plasmoids, egg) are real memories and match mythological evidence, but the dates are unverifiable.

But Cook does not explain how such dates could have been calculated. Without a visible Sun, no solar calendar. Without stars, no sidereal reference. Without a timekeeping device, no way to count days or years. Even if Saturn or Earth had some rotation, Saturn's outer plasma shell would have made it impossible for any life on Earth to observe or record it. The coma obscured everything.

**The first date that can be reliably recorded is 4077 BC.** In that year, energy passing through the system forced the planets to rotate like electric engines. Arc mode ignited. The coma collapsed. The skies cleared. For the first time, there was something to count — a visible Sun, a daily cycle, a mechanism for reckoning time.

We therefore remove anything before 4077 BC from the timeline. The events (coma entry, plasmoids, sub-polar orbit, Cosmic Egg) are still real memories, but it is completely impossible to identify how much time passed between them. The "before creation" period is undatable.

## Creation: 4077 BC

When Saturn **nova'd** in **4077 BC** — transitioning from glow mode to arc mode and blazing suddenly like a sun — Earth's skies cleared for the first time. The Sun became visible. *Regular solar time* began. All founding myths, king lists, and calendrical "year one" dates cluster at or after this event. This is "Creation."
