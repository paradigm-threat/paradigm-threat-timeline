Large electromagnetic fields cause [quantum fluctuation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_fluctuation) reorganizing matter. As plasmoids in the southern sky took new energetic forms, quantum fluctuations on Earth forced similar patterns within a dense, mercury-like liquid metal on the early surface. This conductive substance allowed scalar-driven biogenesis: quantum fluctuations restructured it, shaping the first [Golems](https://www.jmberlin.de/en/topic-golem). Early Golems had no organs (they didn't need to eat, see, or move). Over the Golden Age, sustained scalar energy forced internal differentiation — rudimentary organs forming as metal transmuted into biological tissue. Not Darwinian evolution, but continued external scalar influence.

## The Electric Environment

The scalar field for spontaneous biogenesis was produced by the Saturnian system's sustained plasma engagement. [Jno Cook's chronology](https://saturniancosmology.org/polar.php) proposes a sequence of phases before 4077 BC, but as discussed in [Before Creation](/timeline/evt-before-creation), no timekeeping was possible — these phases are real memories, undatable:

**Before 4077 BC (undatable):** Direct electromagnetic modulation by Saturn. Earth possessed equatorial rings — the **Absu** — suspended up to 8,500 miles above the equator. See [The Absu](/timeline/evt-absu-layers-surrounded-planets). Combined with Saturn's plasma column, Earth was inside a double-layered electromagnetic furnace. Saturn eventually became visible as "the Egg" through thinning plasma (Finnish *Kalevala*: "Ilmatar drifts upon the waters for 700 years" — the duration is mythological, not verifiable).

**4077 BC (Nova Event):** Saturn transitioned from glow to **arc mode**. The coma collapsed instantly, generating peak scalar discontinuity: *"Saturn burst into arc mode, threw out enormous matter, developed a corona, and lit up like a sun."*

## The Sustained Scalar Field: 4077–3147 BC

Following the nova, Earth entered 1,000 years of continuous arc-mode plasma — the **Era of the Gods**. Cook documents: *"Mankind was subjected for a thousand years to a varying magnetic field, a by-product of the rotating Birkeland current."* This oscillating field was not merely therapeutic but *generative*, reorganizing matter at the molecular level.

Mars descended the plasma column repeatedly, each visit accompanied by massive arcing to Earth's surface.
