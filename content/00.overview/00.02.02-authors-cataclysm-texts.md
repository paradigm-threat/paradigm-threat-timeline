# Authors Who Read Ancient Sources as World Cataclysm

Velikovsky (*Worlds in Collision*), de Santillana/von Dechend (*Hamlet's Mill*), the Saturnian school (Talbott, Cardona, Jno Cook), Thornhill, Peratt, Schoch, Hancock, Heinsohn, and others argue ancient myth and scripture are eyewitness records of real planetary catastrophes within the last 8,000 years.
