# The Length of a Year Changes Throughout Antiquity

Venus encounters drove further jumps: Noah's Flood (~2,349 BC, 260 days, Moon appears), Fall of Akkad (~2,193 BC, 273 days), and the Exodus (~1,492 BC, 360 days). The final adjustment came with Mars at ~747 BC (Era of Nabonassar), settling Earth at its current 365.24-day year and 1.00 AU orbit.

No other year-length produces this clean result. Carbon-14 calibration independently corroborates these transitions: shorter-year chronology aligns with INTCAL98 C-14 data, and C-14 "wiggles" track the year-length boundary events.
