# The Length of a Year Changes Throughout Antiquity

Earth's orbital radius — and year-length — changed with each electromagnetic close-encounter. Time-keeping began ~4,077 BC (225-day year); jumps at ~3,147, ~2,349, ~2,193, ~1,492, and ~747 BC produced 240, 260, 273, 360, and 365.24-day years. The Sumerian King-List confirms the 225-day year arithmetically.
