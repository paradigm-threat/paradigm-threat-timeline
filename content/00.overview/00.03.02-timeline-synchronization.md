# Timeline Synchronization

Creation dates across civilizations split into two clusters: the Septuagint-derived dates (~5500 BCE — Antiochian 5969, Byzantine 5508, Alexandrian 5493, Jerome 5199) and the indigenous/pre-Hellenistic dates (~3100–4100 BCE — Ussher 4004, Judaic 3761, Maya Long Count 3114, Hindu Kali Yuga 3102, Egyptian ~3000). These are not independent calculations — they are records controlled by whoever held scribal authority. The Septuagint cluster was set by Hellenistic and Byzantine Church chronologists working backward from theological assumptions; the indigenous cluster preserves dates from traditions that predate centralized record-keeping. The gap between them (~1,400–2,800 years) is not a mystery — it is the fingerprint of redaction.

When the redaction is removed, the dates collapse. Jno Cook's reconstruction shows that different civilizations set their "year zero" at different chapters of the same ~8,000-year sequence: the Quiche Maya counted from 4077 BCE (Saturn goes nova — "sun first shows itself"), the Maya Long Count and Sumerian tradition from 3147 BCE (World Flood — polar configuration collapses), the Biblical/Babylonian tradition from 2349 BCE (Fall of the Absu — "flood of Noah"), and recorded history begins at 685 BCE (end of Mars-Earth conflicts — skies go quiet). These are not contradictions but four snapshots of the same process. The confusion in the original sources arises because later editors — who no longer understood planetary catastrophism — treated each culture's year-zero as a rival "creation date" rather than a chapter marker in a shared sequence. Once you restore the sequence, all dates synchronize into a single coherent narrative spanning 4077–685 BCE.

The reason this synchronization is not widely recognized is that the conquerors burned the evidence: Diego de Landa destroyed virtually all Maya codices at Maní in 1562, the Library of Alexandria burned multiple times, Babylonian astronomical records were discarded as "unreliable" after the fall of Nineveh in 621 BCE, and Aztec codices were systematically burned after 1521. Every tradition that independently confirmed the sequence was targeted. What survives are the fragments the Church could not reach — the Popol Vuh, the Kali Yuga date, the Long Count — and they all point to the same window.
