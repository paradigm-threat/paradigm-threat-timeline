# Timeline Synchronization

Creation dates across civilizations split into two clusters (Septuagint ~5500 BCE vs. indigenous ~3100–4100 BCE). The gap is the fingerprint of redaction. Jno Cook's reconstruction shows different civilizations set year-zero at different chapters of the same sequence: 4077 BCE (Saturn nova), 3147 BCE (World Flood), 2349 BCE (Noah), 685 BCE (history begins). For full date tables and what the conquerors burned: [Timeline Synchronization: Date Tables](/timeline/evt-timeline-synchronization-tables) in the Appendix.
