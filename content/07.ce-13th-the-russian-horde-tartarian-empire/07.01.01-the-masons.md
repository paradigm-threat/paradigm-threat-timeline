## The Temple Commission

Christ's declaration "Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up" (John 2:19) is a [commission recorded by multiple civilizations](https://chronologia.org/en/how_it_was/05_26.html). If Solomon's Temple is [Hagia Sophia](https://chronologia.org/en/how_it_was/06_11.html) — as Fomenko argues, identifying Solomon with Sultan Suleiman — then the "rebuilding" launched a monumental construction programme across the empire.

## One Empire, One Building Programme

Fomenko places the world's most spectacular "ancient" monuments within the same [200-year window (XIV–XVI cc.)](https://chronologia.org/en/how_it_was/04_10.html): Great Wall, Gothic cathedrals, Russian Kremlins, Hagia Sophia, the Sphinx, pyramids of Egypt and Central America, Baalbek. One **programme** — geopolymer cement, megalithic fitting, advanced geometry, continental logistics — not scattered coincidence.
