# Historical Christ dies

> **TL;DR:** **Christ and Isukiri were buried in Japan** — brother swap, eastward retirement, death at **106** (~**1258 CE** with Fomenko birth **1152**). **Shingo (Kirisuto no Haka) is not proved as that locus:** Takeuchi's 1935 tagging is **untrusted** (possible Western-agent channel); original graves likely **destroyed** in the Smithsonian Project and later mound scrubs. See [Alleged Resurrection](/timeline/evt-the-brother-on-the-cross) and the [Takenouchi investigation](https://paradigmthreat.net/history/chronology/investigations/takenouchi-documents-japan-horde-christ-burial-investigation).

## Japan — burial (author thesis)

Historical Christ **survived** the Istanbul crucifixion; **Isukiri** died on the cross. Christ's family carried the body **east** through Siberia into Japan's maritime Horde reach. He lived to **106** and was buried **in Japan** beside his brother — not in Palestine, not in the Western canon.

**1152 + 106 = 1258 CE** — Horde consolidation band. Japan held the **original eastern Christianity** before English/Latin redaction split it into the Western religion-families.

**What we cannot prove:** the **exact coordinates** of those burials today. The **Smithsonian Project** (late 18th / early 19th century; U.S. institutional arm from **1846** — see [MudFlood / giant erasure](https://paradigmthreat.net/history/mudflood)) destroyed **giant-line burial mounds** across Eurasia and the Americas; Japanese **kofun** and mounds were targeted again in later waves. The **original Christ/Isukiri graves in Japan were almost certainly erased** along with other giant tombs. Excavation of any surviving mound today would likely find **normal human remains, empty fill, or replacement material** — not a giant skeleton that would contradict the Palestine narrative.

## Shingo — Takeuchi relay, not proved locus

The village of [Shingo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shing%C5%8D,_Aomori) (Herai) was **publicly tagged** in **1935** by **Kiyomaro Takeuchi** as *Kirisuto no haka*. Villagers had **no prior Christ oral tradition** — predictable after centuries of suppression. The site may have been **ordinary burial mounds** before Takeuchi; the twin-mound Christ reading and festival layer are largely **1930s–1960s packaging**.

**Takeuchi** is read as **redactor** and possible **Western-aligned agent** (witting or not) operating in the interest of **militarized Imperial Japan** ahead of WWII — his **1935 location announcement cannot be trusted** as proof of where Christ died. Kano's *gisho* verdict and post-war debunk framing may be **containment** of a visible but **misdirected** site.

The **sign text** (post-Takeuchi) still preserves useful **narrative geography** — Japan study years, brother swap, eastward flight, age 106 — using **old toponymy and old chronology** (not Scaligerian Palestine). It does **not** prove Shingo is the grave.

> *"When Jesus Christ was 21 years old, he came to Japan and pursued knowledge of divinity for 12 years. He went back to Judea at age 33, and after his crucifixion, his brother Isukiri took his place and Jesus who escaped from the execution traversed the land of Siberia to Alaska, and came to the Aomori Place, where he died at the age of 106."*
