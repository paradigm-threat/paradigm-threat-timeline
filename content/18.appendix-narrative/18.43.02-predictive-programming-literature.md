## H.G. Wells (1866–1946): The Template

Wells set the pattern in the same window as the **[1896–1897 mystery airship wave](/timeline/evt-1896-1897-mystery-airship-wave)** — America's first mass UAP-scale press episode under **airship** vocabulary (because *UFO* did not exist yet), then Mars fiction within months ([vocabulary-bridge article](/influence/controlled_opposition/mystery-airships-1896-1897-vocabulary-bridge.md)). *Crystal Egg* (1897): a crystal sphere provides real-time visual surveillance of Mars—the observer on Earth watches Martians; Martians watch back. *"Sent hither from that planet, in order to give the Martians a near view of our affairs."* **Two-way scrying** in line with Tolkien’s Palantíri and **STARGATE**-era remote viewing (program declassified 1995; **Mars** session PDF: [CIA-RDP96-00788R001900760001-9](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP96-00788R001900760001-9.pdf), dossier [Known Contacts with Mars](/timeline/evt-mars-contacts-timeline)). Same logic: guard a realm, deploy the device for harm.

*War of the Worlds* (1898) inverted prior Mars fiction (benign Martians) into invasion narrative. The **heat ray**—invisible, capacitor-driven—matches the U.S. [Active Denial System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Active_Denial_System). The 2017 sequel (*The Massacre of Mankind*) restored an invisible beam one year after Havana syndrome. **Red weed**—a Martian organism that arrived with the incursions and could not survive on Earth—echoes, six years before the timeline’s [American chestnut blight](/timeline/evt-american-chestnut-blight-martian), an unintended interplanetary contamination from the same incursions: different species, same event frame. *War in the Air* (1908): aerial bombing a decade early. *The World Set Free* (1914): coined *atomic bomb* thirty years before Hiroshima. **Fabian insider**; [Florence Deeks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence_Deeks) demonstrated plagiarism of *The Outline of History*. **AU-084:** physiology contradictions (no digestive system vs “provisions” bipeds; 4ft fungoid vs ~6ft humanoids) are a **self-sealing detailed read** (host/occupant), **not** proof the book was cut — [analysis](/cosmos/mars/investigations/wotw-martian-analysis.md#bipeds-as-occupants--the-provisions-cover).

**Cross-cutting investigation:** [Spore / aerial biological disaster — Martian cylinders, predictive programming, redacted fiction](/history/mudflood/investigations/spore-ecological-disaster-martian-cylinders-investigation.md) — fiction corpus and master register (Wells through Ballard, Disch, Bradbury, Pern, Nausicaä), blood rain, chestnut parallel, PP contrasts (§M–P).

### The Plattner Story (1896) and Davidson's Eyes (1895)

*Plattner Story*: a chemistry teacher enters a **fourth spatial dimension** via explosion; he returns **mirror-reversed** (heart on right)—dimensional displacement. *Davidson's Eyes*: spontaneous remote viewing—a man in London sees in real time through a lighthouse keeper on a South Sea island. Both normalize interdimensional and remote-viewing conceits as fantasy long before U.S. government admission.

## William Goldman (1931–2018)

*The Princess Bride* (novel 1973; film 1987) carries the same family of moves as earlier encoded literature: **displaced authorship** (S. Morgenstern / “good parts” abridger), childhood obsession and parent→child relay of the text, and dialogue that reads forward against later mass face-covering (Fezzik / Man in Black: everybody in masks “in the near future,” “terribly comfortable”). André the Giant as Fezzik intersects the colossus / Andrey naming cluster explored in the full file.

The narrator obtains his own copy, reads to his son, and discovers his father had skipped and edited Morgenstern aloud—the “original” was too dull; the character Goldman then publishes an abridgement that cuts boring stretches and keeps action and intrigue. **Redaction is narrated as family editorial tradition**: the printed book is not the whole posited text—shape of a confession under the joke. Full analysis: [Princess Bride investigation](https://paradigmthreat.net/influence/predictive_programming/fiction_encoding/princess-bride/index-princess-bride-andronicus-fezzik-investigation.md).

Decades after World War II, the public quarrel with myth-making and documentary-framed fiction (Wells, Tolkien, and parallel fights over who owns science and history, including figures such as Einstein) had faded from living memory. **Predictive programming in print could proceed without the same visible establishment backlash** that marked the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries.

By the 1970s, successful, connected writers—Goldman had major screen success after *Butch Cassidy and the Sundance Kid* (1969) and Knopf-tier novel placement—sit where **institutional favors** may be called in: multi-category encoding in one popular package. Sole authorship as the complete explanation is treated as unsettled; circumstantial overlap points to a mass-publishing layer that promotes and buries according to agenda, not merit alone. Full argument, key findings, and evidence checks: [Princess Bride — Andronicus, Fezzik, COVID mask line, Morgenstern frame (investigation)](https://paradigmthreat.net/influence/predictive_programming/fiction_encoding/princess-bride/index-princess-bride-andronicus-fezzik-investigation.md).

Orthodoxy treats S. Morgenstern as pure fiction. The open investigation holds it very likely that Morgenstern was real (or named a real lost line of text) and that Goldman would not have risked the 1973 book if a **checkable Morgenstern edition** could still surface. Whoever cleared the project would need confidence that the underlying work was gone from circulation—finally eliminated—before releasing a replacement framed as abridgement and meta-joke. That window coincides with room for a new “historic fantasy” genre shell: copyright fiction, ironic distance, no public compare to a missing original. [Investigation — Key findings §5 and Conclusion subsection](https://paradigmthreat.net/influence/predictive_programming/fiction_encoding/princess-bride/index-princess-bride-andronicus-fezzik-investigation.md).

The novel flatters impatience with anything that is not immediate plot: reasons, kingdoms, traditions, Morgenstern’s historical satire—all filed as dull unless the father or abridger cuts to action. Ancient or dense matter is framed as boring from a small boy’s point of view; the text then serves torture, court intrigue, and war as entertainment rather than as material the reader is asked to place in real historical coordinates. **Identification is explicit**: the audience stands with the child listener who only wants to be amused and to skip the heavy parts. On this site’s working reading, that joke overlaps a model of mass readership as *anti-curious*—thrills over inquiry—while the “boring” layers remain where redacted history can hide. [Key findings §6; Anti-intellectual frame section](https://paradigmthreat.net/influence/predictive_programming/fiction_encoding/princess-bride/index-princess-bride-andronicus-fezzik-investigation.md).

If stakeholders bet on no surviving Morgenstern original, random obscurity is a weak explanation for that confidence. May 1933: Nazi Germany—mass public book burnings, including Berlin’s Bebelplatz, destroyed large stocks of targeted works—[Nazi book burnings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi_book_burnings). Post-1945: German denazification and Italian / Allied purges—e.g. Allied Order No. 4 (13 May 1946), removal and destruction of fascist, militarist, and Nazi literature from libraries, schools, and bookstores—extend the same class of mechanisms. Hypothesis (no archival Morgenstern hit cited yet): a real line might have been lost in 1933 and/or caught in postwar sweeps, freeing a fictional Morgenstern abridgement—**“perfect crime” shape** against provenance. Florin is set **between where Sweden and Germany would eventually settle**, with parenthetical jokes about “before Europe” and “after Paris.” **Plot sovereignty** still sits in **Florin, Guilder, Florin City**, and the Channel.

**User sentiment (captured here):** That mix does not read as a sealed secondary world; it reads as **real history and real maps with a mask**. The author **majorly padded** the invented realm with **real locations and real historical texture**, which makes the **fictional kingdoms more suspicious, not more grounded**—as if the text **trained** the reader’s mental map on Earth and then **swapped** the crown labels. Chronological collage is **advertised**, not hidden. Mainstream reading: comic anachronism.
