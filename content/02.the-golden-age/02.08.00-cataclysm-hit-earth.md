# Cataclysm hit Earth

> **TL;DR:** **Mars** **inner-shell** **approaches** **strike** **between** **retreats**, etching **Fenrir**, **cosmic** **mountain**, and **Osiris** **wound** **myth**; the **civilization-scale** **break** is the **~3147** **Great** **Deluge** through **Saturn’s** **salt** **sheath**, **separate** from **earlier** **undated** **contacts**.

Mars's inner shell, when it approached Earth in oscillation, sometimes caused cataclysm before retreating. These encounters are recorded in myth (Osiris, Fenris, cosmic mountain). The definitive cataclysm ending the Golden Age was the Great Deluge (~3147 BCE), when Earth and other planets passed through Saturn's outer plasma sheath and sodium-salt water crashed onto the surface. Earlier Mars-approach cataclysms during the Golden Age are not dated by Jno Cook or Talbott.
