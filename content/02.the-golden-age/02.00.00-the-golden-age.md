Age*. The Hindu Brahma, Yama, Vishnu, and Manu describe the age of the [“Brilliant Yima”](https://saturniancosmology.org/files/blog/blog-michael1.txt).

In the Christian Bible, this 930-year period was the [Garden of Eden](https://bennettleeross.com/history/intruder-alert-a-fast-approaching-brown-dwarf-star/).
The Caribs of Surinam recall the lost epoch: "In a time long past, so long past that even the grandmothers of our grandmothers were not yet born, the world was quite other than what it is today: the trees were forever in fruit; the animals lived in perfect harmony, and the little agouti played fearlessly with the beard of the jaguar." The cosmic mountain appears under many names: Meru (Hindu), Hera-Berezaiti (Iranian), Kwen-Lun (Chinese), Colhuacan (Mexican), Olympus and Zion (Talbott, *The Saturn Myth*).

As each planet was connected [in-series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Series_and_parallel_circuits#Series_circuits),

this period, each planet in the configuration had become a "fully charged" capacitor, unable to accept more charge or
grow larger. The configuration violently came to an end, and the age of cataclysm began.

## The 225-Day Year

*Evidence from the Sumerian King-List*

Saturn was called [Kronos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cronus), the *God before time began*. Before clocks and calendars, planetary positions were the only way to measure time. When Saturn's system joined the Sun's orbit, time began. Our current Sun was still hidden behind Saturn (and Jupiter). Earth's spin remained constant — a "day" stayed the same — but the year was only 225 days: 40% faster than today's 365.25-day orbit. Year-lengths shifted periodically as Earth jumped between orbits, each jump drawing less solar energy and producing a slower orbit. Saturn or Kronos translates to "time." The visible crescent of Saturn — lit by the real Sun at Saturn's rim — was the first clock. Earth's rotation made it appear to turn; Talbott's revolving-crescent article details the four-phase daily cycle. When Jupiter displaced Saturn after 3147 BC, Jupiter replaced "the older God of Time and Beginnings." Sumerian King-List (*List of Kings Before the Flood*) — reigns in tens of thousands of "years" dismissed as fantastical. Cook: these record *days*; only a 225-day year resolves totals into whole-number multiples of 32 years. Grand total: 241,200 days = 1,072 years. A 225-day year = 0.72 AU (Venus's orbit); Kepler's third law gives ~214 days for 0.70 AU — consistent. Full tables: [Year-Length and Calendar Evidence](/timeline/evt-year-length-calendar-evidence).

## Saturn Collinear Configuration (Tree of Life)

Until the moment of '*creation*', Earth had been hiding like a seedling safely inside Saturn's nurturing brown-dwarf system. Birkeland currents forced the planets into a new *collinear configuration*, connecting each planet from north to south pole, like batteries. In *The Saturn Myth* David Talbott writes:

> "Accounts of Saturn's appearance suggest that the planet hung ominously close to the earth. In early ritual and astronomy Saturn appears as the primeval sun, described as a figure of terrifying splendour. Today, Saturn appears as a bare speck of light following the same visual path as the solar orb. But during the legendary Golden Age, Saturn stood in the north. Legends from every continent depict the primeval sun as an immense, fiery globe at the north celestial pole—the visual pivot of the heavens."
> — *David Talbott, [The Saturn Myth](https://www.everythingselectric.com/product/the-saturn-myth/) (1980)*
