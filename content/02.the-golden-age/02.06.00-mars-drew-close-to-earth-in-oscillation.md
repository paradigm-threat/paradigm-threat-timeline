# Mars drew close to Earth in oscillation

Priori-Mars was hollow, with outer shells of decreasing density. During interplanetary encounters, the outer shell could collapse and the inner shell would exit, forming a long streaking cone (Yggdrasil, Tower of Babel). The inner shell would approach Earth, sometimes causing cataclysm, before retreating and returning. This oscillation repeated throughout the Golden Age and Dark Ages. When the first such close approach occurred is not dated. Jno Cook and Talbott reference the cosmic mountain and Mars's eight descents (Osiris/Triform God) but do not assign specific years.
