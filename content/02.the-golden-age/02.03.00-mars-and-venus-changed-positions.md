# Mars and Venus changed positions

The northern hemisphere configuration placed Mars and Venus in a fixed order: Mars closest to Earth, then Venus, then Saturn. Talbott and Thornhill describe Mars as Fenrir (solid sphere) and Venus as the Midgard Serpent/Dragon (plasmoid). When these positions changed—whether gradually, at instability, or at the breakup (~3147 BCE)—is not dated. After the collinear configuration ended, the planets "violently competed" for new orbits; the Mars–Venus "battle" (Fenris vs Midgard) is remembered in the Dark Ages.
