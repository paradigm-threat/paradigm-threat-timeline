# Earth started rotating visibly

During the collinear configuration, planets in the northern chain (Mars, Venus, Saturn, Jupiter) were aligned pole-to-pole and did not spin independently. Earth's orientation was fixed toward Saturn. In [*The Saturn Myth*](https://www.everythingselectric.com/product/the-saturn-myth/) David Talbott describes Saturn as fixed at the north celestial pole—the visual pivot of the heavens.

[Jno Cook](https://saturniancosmology.org/time.php) describes the moment the crescent became the day/night marker:

> "As the Earth rotated (below Saturn), the crescent appeared to move counterclockwise around the outer rim of the globe on a daily basis. The crescent dimmed as it moved up toward the 'top' of Saturn during the Earth's daylight hours, and brightened again as it dipped down to the bottom at night. The first experience of clock-time had started for humanity."
> — *[Jno Cook, saturniancosmology.org/time.php](https://saturniancosmology.org/time.php)*

Whether Earth *began* rotating then or had always rotated and 4077 marks when the crescent became the day/night marker is ambiguous. Talbott does not assign dates.
