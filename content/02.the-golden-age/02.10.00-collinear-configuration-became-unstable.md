# Collinear configuration became unstable

Each planet in the configuration had been absorbing matter and energy from the Sun, growing in size. Eventually they became too large and too energetic, began to oscillate violently in a new non-linear orbit, and the collinear system broke up. The Golden Age ended. Planets exited Saturn's protective plasma sheath and violently competed for positions. Jupiter displaced Saturn; the Great Deluge followed. The Maya Long Count calendar marks Earth's jump to a 240-day orbit in 3114 BCE. Sources: Jno Cook, David Talbott, paradigm-threat-files chronology.
