Extended evidence for [World War II: Reverse Crusade III](/timeline/evt-world-war-ii-reverse-crusade-iii): architectural erasure, German evacuation thesis, Vril question, Mars testimony, and twin-tower biological control investigation.

## The Destruction of Old-World Architecture

Allied firebombing targeted irreplaceable pre-modern architecture — Dresden, Hamburg, Cologne, Tokyo, Osaka. The [Mudflood thesis](https://files.paradigmthreat.net/history/mudflood) documents Tartarian-style buildings with buried first floors and anomalous construction. WW2 completed what the Expositions began: razed entire cities, replaced with modernist concrete. See Howdie Mickoski, *Exposing the Expositions*.

## The German Evacuation Thesis

**Neuschwabenland / Base 211** — 1938–39 German Antarctic expedition; alternative account holds Base 211 established. **Operation Highjump (1946–47)** — Byrd deployed 4,700 personnel, 13 ships, 33 aircraft. Byrd (El Mercurio, March 1947): *"In case of a new war, the continental US would be attacked by flying objects which could fly from pole to pole at incredible speeds."* Karl Unger letter (U-209) — unverified. German evacuation narrative points to inner Earth and Mars.
