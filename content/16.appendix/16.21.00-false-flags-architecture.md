The following documented statements support the advance-knowledge thesis. See the main article for the operational summary (McCollum Memo, Purple code, missing carriers).

## Battle of Los Angeles (February 24–25, 1942)

Whatever was in the sky over Los Angeles that night survived 1,400+ anti-aircraft rounds without damage, did not drop bombs, and was not Japanese. Japan confirmed post-war they flew no aircraft over Los Angeles. Five civilians died (three in car crashes, two of heart attacks). The official explanation — weather balloons — is the same explanation offered for Roswell five years later. (See [Mars Contacts Timeline](/timeline/evt-mars-contacts-timeline).)

## The Nuclear Question

Before Hiroshima (August 6, 1945), sixty-six Japanese cities had been area-bombed with napalm. Tokyo alone (March 10, 1945): 100,000 killed, 270,000 buildings destroyed. Hiroshima and Nagasaki were **deliberately left unbombed** prior to the atomic attacks to provide undamaged demonstration targets. Survivor symptoms — progressive wasting, generational effects — are more consistent with depleted uranium contamination than acute fission radiation. The Tsar Bomba (1961) may have served as a narrative countermeasure: having insisted fission bombs could incinerate cities, the West could not deny the Soviet claim of a fusion bomb, producing Mutually Assured Destruction — a strategic equilibrium.
