# “New World” naming, MFEE, and colonization memory

This appendix summarizes an **open investigation** in paradigm-threat-files: whether language such as **Mundus Novus** / the **New World**, later **New** Holland, **New** Zealand, **New** England, and rhetoric of **empty** or **discoverable** land could mean **more** than European expansion branding — specifically whether it might **also** track **terrain repopulated after** the [MudFlood Energetic Event](/timeline/evt-the-mudflood-world-cataclysm), with official history later **splicing** that story to the **Columbus / 1492** exodus frame (Reconquista, Ottoman pressure, Inquisition) so the tie to **cataclysm** drops out.

Mainstream etymology (European novelty; New Zealand from Dutch **Zeeland**) is **not** discarded in the investigation; the question is **layered** meaning and **map discontinuities** after ~1774.
