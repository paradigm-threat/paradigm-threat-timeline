## Plot Summary

Arthur's life from boyhood (Wart, tutored by Merlin) through kingship, the Round Table, Lancelot and Guinevere, the Grail quest, Mordred's betrayal, and Arthur's death. White modernizes Malory with 20th-century idiom, comedy, and themes of war, human nature, and governance. The Round Table—knights in fellowship around a circular table—is central.
