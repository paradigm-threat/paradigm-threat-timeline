# The Curse of Lono — Index

**Author:** Hunter S. Thompson (with Steve Crist, Ralph Steadman, Sean Penn)  
**Published:** 1983 (serial); 2005 TASCHEN limited collector's edition  
**Full text:** `Curse-of-Lono.mobi`

---

## Overview

Thompson's account of covering the Honolulu Marathon (1980) and a fishing expedition in Hawaii. Blends sportswriting, travel, and gonzo excess. Steadman illustrations throughout.

---

## Government-Antagonistic Themes

- **Hawaii and empire** — Statehood, tourism, military presence; Lono (Hawaiian god) as symbol of displaced indigenous authority
- **Sport and spectacle** — Marathon as cultural ritual; who benefits
- **Environment** — Fishing, ocean; extraction vs. stewardship
