## Overview

Volume of Thompson's letters and correspondence from the 1960s–2000s. Covers his career, politics, editors, and the evolution of gonzo. Complements the journalism in *Fear and Loathing in Las Vegas* and *Great Shark Hunt*.

---

## Government-Antagonistic Themes

- **Direct statements** — Letters may contain unfiltered views on Nixon, war, police, media
- **Editorial relationships** — Rolling Stone, Jann Wenner; what got published vs. spiked
- **Career context** — How Thompson positioned himself; institutional access and resistance
