# False Flags: 20th–21st Century Catalog

Extended catalog of known, confirmed, and suspected false flag operations. Complements the [Appendix: False Flags Investigation Evidence Dossier](/timeline/evt-false-flags-appendix). Status key: **Confirmed** = declassified/official acknowledgement; **Suspected** = strong evidence, contested; **Alleged** = theory, unconfirmed.

---

## Confirmed / Documented

### Gleiwitz Incident (1939)
Nazi SS operatives dressed as Polish soldiers attacked the Gleiwitz radio station on 31 August 1939; left concentration camp prisoners in Polish uniforms (killed by lethal injection) as "evidence." Part of **Operation Himmler** (Reinhard Heydrich, Heinrich Müller) — coordinated false flags along the Polish border. Hitler cited it on 1 September to justify invading Poland; Britain and France declared war 3 September. Declassified Nazi documents and postwar testimony confirm orchestration.

**Sources:** Wikipedia [Gleiwitz incident](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gleiwitz_incident), [Operation Himmler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Himmler).

### Mukden / Manchurian Incident (1931)
Japanese Kwantung Army officers staged a small explosion on the South Manchurian Railway near Mukden, blamed Chinese saboteurs. Japan used it to occupy Manchuria and establish Manchukuo. Officer testimony confirmed the staging.

**Sources:** Appendix dossier; Wikipedia [Mukden Incident](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mukden_incident).

### Shelling of Mainila (1939)
Soviet Red Army shelled the Soviet village of Mainila near the Finnish border on 26 November 1939, then falsely claimed Finland had fired. Finnish observation posts showed shells detonated ~800 m inside Soviet territory; Finnish artillery was out of range. Soviet daily reports showed no personnel losses. Soviet archives (Andrei Zhdanov; Khrushchev's memoirs) later attributed the shelling to Artillery Marshal Grigory Kulik. Finland proposed neutral investigation; USSR refused, renounced non-aggression pact, launched Winter War 30 November. Boris Yeltsin (1994) denounced the Winter War as Soviet aggression.

**Sources:** Wikipedia [Shelling of Mainila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shelling_of_Mainila).

### Gulf of Tonkin (1964)
Second alleged North Vietnamese attack on USS Maddox never occurred. NSA documents declassified 2005 confirmed fabrication. LBJ used it for Gulf of Tonkin Resolution; ~58,000 U.S. deaths in Vietnam. Daniel Ellsberg knew; leaked Pentagon Papers (1971).

**Sources:** Appendix dossier; NSA declassified docs (2005).

### Lavon Affair (1954)
Israeli agents bombed Egyptian civilian targets (cinemas, post offices), disguised as Muslim Brotherhood. Exposed when operatives caught. Israel acknowledged 2005.

**Sources:** Appendix dossier.

### Operation Gladio / Bologna Massacre (1960s–1990s; Bologna 1980)
NATO-backed "stay behind" network conducted bombings across Europe, blamed on left-wing groups. **Bologna station bombing (2 Aug 1980):** 85 dead, 200+ wounded; neo-fascist NAR convicted; P2 lodge (Licio Gelli) and Gladio connections established in 2020s prosecutions. Italian judge Felice Casson; Vincenzo Vinciguerra testimony.

**Sources:** Appendix dossier; Wikipedia [Bologna massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bologna_massacre).

## Suspected / Debated

### USS Maine (1898)
Explosion in Havana Harbor; blast blew outward (internal), not inward (mine). Spain helped rescue survivors. Hearst/Pulitzer media campaign. Triggered Spanish-American War.

**Sources:** Appendix dossier.

### Reichstag Fire (1933)
German parliament burned 27 Feb 1933; Nazis blamed communists; Reichstag Fire Decree suspended civil liberties. Van der Lubbe convicted; scholarly debate on Nazi involvement (Oberfohren Memorandum; Tobias forgery claims).

**Sources:** Appendix dossier.

### Lusitania (1915)
British liner torpedoed by U-boat; ~1,197 dead, 128 Americans. Ship carried war supplies. Wartime propaganda blamed Germany; contemporary investigations obstructed. Contributed to U.S. entry into WWI (1917).

**Sources:** Appendix dossier (extended article); Wikipedia [Sinking of the RMS Lusitania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinking_of_the_RMS_Lusitania).

### Pearl Harbor (1941)
McCollum Memo; Purple code broken; carriers at sea; Stimson diary. Advance-knowledge thesis; Kimmel/Short scapegoated.

**Sources:** Appendix dossier (full evidentiary record).

### King David Hotel (1946)
Irgun (Menachem Begin) bombed British HQ in Jerusalem; 91 dead; disguised as Arab workers.

**Sources:** Appendix dossier.

### USS Liberty (1967)
Israel attacked U.S. ship during Six-Day War; 34 dead. Israel claimed mistaken identity; survivors maintain deliberate attack.

**Sources:** Appendix dossier.

### Operation Northwoods (1962)
U.S. Joint Chiefs proposed staging terrorist attacks on U.S. soil, blaming Cuba. JFK rejected. Declassified 1997.

**Sources:** Appendix dossier.

### 1972 Munich Olympics
Black September killed 11 Israeli athletes; security failures; political consequences aligned with Israeli/Western objectives.

**Sources:** Appendix dossier.

### Oklahoma City (1995)
Murrah Building; 168 dead. McVeigh convicted. Multiple witnesses reported additional devices; security footage confiscated.

**Sources:** Appendix dossier.

### 9/11 (2001)
See [9/11 Investigation](09-11-investigation.md). Good-actor historic antibody thesis; WTC 7 and CIA station.

## Alleged / 21st Century

### Syria Chemical Weapons (2013 Ghouta; 2018 Douma)
Rebel-held areas; chlorine/sarin alleged. Russia and Syria claim staging/false flag. OPCW 2023 report blamed Syrian Tiger Forces for Douma. Counter-narrative: OPCW leaker "Alex"; Ian Henderson engineering report (cylinders placed, not dropped). Contested.

**Sources:** Bellingcat; CounterPunch; OPCW reports.

### Russia–Ukraine (2022–2026)
Institute for the Study of War and Western sources allege Russian false-flag plans (e.g. nuclear staging in Ukraine) to justify escalation. Kremlin denies. Unconfirmed.

**Sources:** ISW; news reports.

### Iran 2026 (Minab School Strike)
See [Iran 2026 Propaganda Thesis](iran-2026-propaganda-thesis.md). **Project theory:** Iran may be hitting its own schools and staging "training missions" as propaganda during war. Mainstream narrative: U.S./Israel strike; both deny.
