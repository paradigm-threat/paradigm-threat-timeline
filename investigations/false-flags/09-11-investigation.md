## Sponsored Theories: Controlled Demolition and No Planes

This investigation **sponsors** (does not assert as proven):

### Controlled Demolition
- WTC 1, 2, and 7 exhibited collapse dynamics consistent with controlled demolition: near-free-fall acceleration; symmetrical failure; dust clouds; lateral ejections.
- WTC 7 was **not hit by an aircraft**; NIST's "fire-induced collapse" explanation remains contested.
- The towers' steel-frame design was built to withstand aircraft impact (post-1945 design standards).

### No Planes / Plane CGI Added Later
- Eyewitness accounts of "explosions" and "no planes" have been documented.
- The possibility that **plane footage was CGI added to broadcast and archival records** is treated as a live hypothesis — i.e., the physical event may have involved explosives and/or other means, with plane imagery inserted into media after the fact to reinforce the official narrative.
- We do not dismiss this on grounds of "impossibility"; we document it as part of the evidentiary landscape.
