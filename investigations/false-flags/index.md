## Core Thesis: False Flags on Both Sides

Every war requires false flags from **both** combatants. The pattern is not unique to the Deep State: it is the **structural logic of war itself**. Sun Tzu: *"All warfare is based on deception."* To mobilise populations, legitimise aggression, and obscure the true objectives of elites, both sides manufacture narratives that serve their ends. The sinking of the USS Maine, Pearl Harbor, Gulf of Tonkin, and 9/11 follow the same architecture — but **who benefits** determines whether a false flag advances or undermines a given faction's plans.

**Project stance:** 9/11 may represent a **historic antibody** — a false flag executed by good actors that *sabotaged* the Deep State's slow-and-go plan to use simulated terrorism to infiltrate governments and religions. See [9/11 Investigation](09-11-investigation.md).
